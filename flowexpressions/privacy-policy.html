Flow Expressions — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026 · describes Flow Expressions 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Flow Expressions collects nothing and stores nothing about you. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.

What the app stores

Nothing. The expression reference and the recipes are built into the app. What you type in the search box is used only to filter the list on screen and is not saved; it is gone when you close the app.

The clipboard

When you tap a Copy button, the app places that expression text on your phone's clipboard so you can paste it elsewhere. The app never reads your clipboard.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions. It does not request internet, storage, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar or location access.

Deleting your data

There is no data to delete. Uninstalling the app removes it completely.

Children

The app is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, children included, and has no ads, links to other apps or ways to contact other people.

Not affiliated with Microsoft

Flow Expressions is an independent reference made by OVC Tech. Power Automate and Azure Logic Apps are trademarks of Microsoft, which does not endorse this app.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above (for example by adding advertising), this page will say so before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

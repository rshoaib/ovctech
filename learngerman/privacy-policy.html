Learn German: Phrases & Words — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026 · describes Learn German 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Learn German collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Your progress lives only on your phone.

What the app stores, on your phone only

  • Which phrases you have marked as learned, which you have starred as favourites, your settings (speaking speed, whether flashcards are read aloud) and a short history of practice sessions (date, topic, score) used to show your day streak.
  • This is kept in the app's private storage on the device. Nothing is uploaded, synced or shared.
  • The app turns off Android cloud backup, so the system does not copy its data to your Google account. Uninstalling the app deletes the data; "Reset progress" in Settings clears it too.

Speech

Phrases are spoken by the text-to-speech engine already installed on your phone (for example Google Speech Services or your phone maker's engine). The app hands the engine the phrase text and nothing else; the engine runs under its own maker's privacy policy. The app does not record audio and has no microphone permission.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not request any other permission: no microphone, camera, location, contacts, files or notifications.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not ask for your name, email or any account.

Contacting us

The Settings screen offers a WhatsApp and an email button. Tapping one opens WhatsApp or your mail app with a message addressed to us; nothing is sent until you choose to send it there, and those apps handle the message under their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is a general-audience language learning tool aimed at adults (18 and up). It is not designed for children and collects no data from anyone.

Accuracy

The phrases were written and checked by hand. If you find a mistake, tell us from the Settings screen and it is corrected in the next update.

Changes

If a future version ever collects data (for example, if advertising is added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the Google Play data-safety section will say so.

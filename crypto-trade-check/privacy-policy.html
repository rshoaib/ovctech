📊 Crypto Trade Check — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Introduction

Crypto Trade Check ("we", "our", "the app") is a free crypto market-analysis tool developed and published by Rizwan Shoaib. The app reads public cryptocurrency market data from Binance and runs a set of transparent technical checks on your device to produce a Long / Short / No-trade read. It has no user accounts, no ads, and no analytics. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with data.

Data We Access

  • Public market data — the app connects to Binance's public, keyless USD-M futures market-data endpoints to read candlestick history, prices, funding, open interest and order-book depth for the trading pair you select. These are anonymous, read-only public price feeds. No personal information is sent with these requests.
  • Your Binance API key (optional) — you may choose to paste a read-only Binance API key so the app can show your own open position and balance alongside the market read. This is entirely optional; every other feature works without it. See the dedicated section below.
  • Settings and history you create — the pair you are watching, your alert preferences, favourites, and the trade journal you build up are stored locally on your device.

Your Binance API Key

If you enter one, the key and secret are stored using your device's hardware-backed secure storage (Android Keystore), not in ordinary app storage.

  • The key is used only to sign read requests sent directly from your phone to Binance, for two endpoints: your position risk and your wallet balance.
  • The key and secret are never sent to us, never sent to our server, and never sent to any third party other than Binance itself.
  • The app cannot place, modify, or cancel orders, cannot withdraw or transfer funds, and cannot hold or custody any asset. It has no wallet.
  • The app asks you to create the key with reading permission only — no trading and no withdrawal rights.
  • You can remove the key at any time from the app's Binance settings screen, which deletes it from secure storage.

Alert Notifications and Our Server

The app can notify you when the market read changes. Android aggressively suspends background work, so if you switch alerts on, the app also registers with a small backend we run (Google Firebase, project "Riz Apps") which repeats the same check and sends the notification by push.

What the app sends when alerts are switched on:

  • Your device's Firebase Cloud Messaging registration token — a device identifier that lets Google's push service deliver a notification to your phone.
  • The trading pair you are watching and your alert preference toggles.
  • A confidence-calibration mapping derived from your own trade journal, so the server thresholds against the same number the app shows you.
  • Your platform (Android) and the app version.

What it never sends: your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, advertising ID, your Binance API key or secret, your balance, or your positions. We have no way to identify you personally from what is stored.

If you never switch alerts on, nothing is ever sent to our server. Switching alerts off tells the server to stop, and the app stops registering.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ We do NOT collect your personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • ❌ We do NOT have user accounts, logins, or profiles
  • ❌ We do NOT track your location
  • ❌ We do NOT sell or share your data with anyone
  • ❌ We do NOT serve ads of any kind
  • ❌ We do NOT use an advertising ID
  • ❌ We do NOT use third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • ❌ We do NOT receive, store, or transmit your exchange API key, secret, balance, or positions

Local Storage

The app stores the following on your device:

  • The active trading pair, your favourites and recent pairs
  • Your alert preferences and alert state
  • Your trade journal and any position you record
  • Cached market calibration data, so the app does not re-download it every run
  • Your Binance API key and secret, if you entered one, in secure hardware-backed storage

You can clear all of it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

  • Internet — Required. Used to read Binance's public market-data endpoints, to read your own account if you supplied a key, and to register for alerts if you switched them on. It is not used for advertising or analytics.
  • Notifications — Optional. Used only to alert you about the trading pair you chose.
  • Run at startup / Wake lock / Vibrate — Used so scheduled alert checks and their notifications work reliably.

The app does not request or use an advertising ID.

Data Retention & Deletion

On your device: local data stays for as long as the app is installed. There is no expiration.

On our server: if you switched alerts on, one record keyed by your push token holds the items listed above. Switching alerts off stops it being used, and uninstalling the app invalidates the push token.

How to delete your data:

  • Go to Settings → Apps → Crypto Trade Check → Storage → Clear Data to erase all locally stored app data including any saved API key, or
  • Uninstall the app, which permanently deletes all associated data from your device, or
  • Email segmentbi@gmail.com to request deletion of the server-side alert registration.

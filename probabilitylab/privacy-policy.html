Probability Lab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026 · describes Probability Lab 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Probability Lab collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Every experiment, chart, explanation and lesson is computed on your phone from numbers the app generates itself.

What the app stores

  • Nothing. The results of a run exist only on screen while you look at them and are gone when you leave the results screen or close the app. There is no saved history and no settings.
  • The app turns off Android cloud backup; there is no app data to back up in any case.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not request any permission at all: no microphone, camera, contacts, location, storage or notifications.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not ask for your name, email or any account.
  • It involves no money, no betting and no real-world stakes of any kind. Coins, dice and cards are simulated as the standard objects of a maths lesson, and the app teaches probability and nothing else.

Children

The app is a general-audience education tool aimed at ages 13 and up (students, teachers and adult learners). It is not designed for children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version ever collects data (for example, if advertising is added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the Google Play data-safety section will say so.

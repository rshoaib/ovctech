Date & Age Calculator — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026 · describes Date & Age Calculator 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Date & Age Calculator collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The dates you type are used for the calculation on screen and are not saved.

What the app stores

  • Nothing. The app has no saved history and no settings. When you close it, the dates you typed (including any date of birth) are gone.
  • The app turns off Android cloud backup; there is no app data to back up in any case.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not request any other permission: no calendar, contacts, camera, microphone, location, files or notifications. It does not read your phone's calendar or contacts.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not ask for your name, email or any account.

Children

The app is a general-audience date utility aimed at ages 13 and up. It is not designed for children and collects no data from anyone.

Accuracy

The app counts Gregorian calendar dates in your phone's time zone using standard calendar rules (whole years, then whole months, then days; ISO 8601 week numbers; weekdays are Monday to Friday and public holidays are not known). For legal deadlines, contracts or official age requirements, check the result against the rule that applies to you.

Changes

If a future version ever collects data (for example, if advertising is added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the Google Play data-safety section will say so.

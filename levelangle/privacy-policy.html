Level & Angle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026 · describes Level & Angle 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Level & Angle collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It reads the phone's motion sensor (accelerometer) to show the tilt on screen; those readings are not saved or shared.

What the app stores

  • Only your calibration offset: two or three small numbers (in degrees) saved in the app's private storage on your phone when you calibrate. Reset in the app, or uninstalling it, deletes them.
  • Sensor readings are used live for the level and are never written to storage.
  • The app turns off Android cloud backup, so the calibration stays on this phone.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not request any runtime permission: no camera, microphone, contacts, location, physical-activity, files or notifications. Reading the accelerometer needs no permission on Android.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not ask for your name, email or any account.

Children

The app is a general-audience measuring tool aimed at ages 13 and up. It is not designed for children and collects no data from anyone.

Accuracy

Readings come from your phone's motion sensor and can be off by a degree or more depending on the phone and its case. Use a proper level or inclinometer for work that must be exact.

Changes

If a future version ever collects data (for example, if advertising is added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the Google Play data-safety section will say so.

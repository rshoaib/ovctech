Animal Rescue Rover — Privacy Policy

Published by Rizwan Shoaib

Last updated: July 22, 2026

The short version

Animal Rescue Rover does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no network connection. It is a simple, fully offline game that runs entirely on your device. This is by design and is the whole point of the app.

What the app is

Animal Rescue Rover is a gentle, endless driving game for children. You steer a little car between lanes to catch falling animals and splash through mud puddles. There is no way to lose and no "game over". The app works completely offline.

Data we do NOT collect

The app collects no personal information whatsoever. Specifically, it does not collect or transmit:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact information
  • Location data
  • Photos, camera, microphone, or media
  • Contacts, calendar, or files
  • Device identifiers or an advertising ID
  • Usage analytics, crash logs, or telemetry of any kind

The app does not request the INTERNET permission and cannot send anything off your device. It requests no runtime permissions at all.

No third-party SDKs

Animal Rescue Rover contains no third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, social, or tracking SDKs. No third party receives any information about you or your child, because the app shares nothing with anyone and has no way to connect to a network.

What is stored on your device

The app saves a single number — your best rescue score (high score) — in the app's own private on-device storage so the game can show it the next time you play. This value never leaves the device, is not linked to you or any identifier, and is deleted if you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

No ads

Animal Rescue Rover contains no advertising and no ad networks. There is no advertising ID, no ad SDK, and no in-app purchases.

Children's privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families)

Animal Rescue Rover is designed to be safe for children and families, and is intended to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families policy.

Because the app collects no data, contains no ads, has no accounts, uses no third-party SDKs, and has no network connection, there is nothing to collect from anyone — including children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect, use, or disclose any personal information from children (or anyone else), and it is technically impossible for the app to do so. No parental consent flow is needed because no personal information is ever requested, gathered, or transmitted.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and the "last updated" date above.

ClarityCam — Privacy Policy

Published by Rizwan Shoaib

Last updated: September 23, 2026

The short version

Your practice videos, their transcripts and your history stay on your phone. There is no account and no server of ours. From version 1.7.0 ClarityCam is free and supported by a single banner ad served by Google AdMob; to request it the app needs internet access and uses your device's advertising ID. That ad request is the only thing that ever leaves your phone, and this policy explains exactly what it involves. Earlier versions carried no ads and made no network requests at all.

What the app records, and where it goes

  • Video and audio. When you press record, the app films you for up to 90 seconds using your phone's camera and microphone. The file is written to ClarityCam's own private storage area on the device. It is not added to your gallery, not shared with other apps, and never uploaded.
  • Speech transcript. While you record, your phone's built-in offline speech recognition converts what you say to text so the app can work out your speaking pace and count filler words. This runs entirely on the device. ClarityCam explicitly requests on-device recognition and never falls back to a network recogniser.
  • Your history. Dates, prompts, word counts, pace and filler figures are saved in the app's own local storage so it can show your streak and trend.

All of this lives only on your phone. None of it is sent to Google, to an advertiser, or to anyone else — the ad request described below carries no recording, transcript or history. Uninstalling ClarityCam deletes every recording and every number along with it, and there is no copy anywhere else to recover from.

Advertising

From version 1.7.0, ClarityCam is free and supported by ads. It displays a single banner ad served by Google AdMob at the bottom of the Today screen. There is no full-screen ad, no ad on launch, and no ad on the recording or result screens.

To request an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app sends the following to Google and its advertising partners:

  • Your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier Android provides for advertising) and app-set ID.
  • Your IP address, from which Google derives an approximate location. ClarityCam never asks for location permission.
  • Coarse device and app information — device model, OS version, app version, and interaction with the ad itself.
  • Diagnostics about the ad request, such as whether it loaded.

This data is collected by Google and shared with Google as an advertising partner. It is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google's handling of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the AdMob partner policies.

Consent in Europe. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, ClarityCam shows a Google-certified consent form the first time it runs and records your choice. If you do not consent to personalised advertising, the app requests non-personalised ads instead. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Ad content is limited to Google's "G" (general audience) rating.

Backups

Android can normally copy an app's data to a user's Google Drive automatically. ClarityCam turns this off (allowBackup=false), so your recordings and transcripts are not included in a device backup.

Permissions, and why each one exists

  • Camera — to film your practice run. Used only while the recording screen is open.
  • Microphone — to record your voice and, via your phone's offline speech recognition, to measure your pace and filler words.
  • Notifications — only if you switch on the optional daily reminder. These are scheduled locally by your phone; no server is involved.
  • Run at startup — so a reminder you set survives a restart.
  • Keep awake — so the screen does not lock in the middle of a 90-second recording.
  • Internet — used solely to request ads. Your recordings, transcripts and history are never sent over it.
  • Advertising ID — used by Google AdMob to serve ads, as described above.

The app does not request location, contacts, or access to your photo library.

Children

ClarityCam is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Third parties

Google AdMob, for the banner ad described above, is the only third party. There is no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party account. Speech recognition is performed by the software already built into your phone, not by a service ClarityCam sends your audio to.

Deleting your data

Your recordings and history live on your device only, so there is nothing for us to retain, delete or hand over. Delete recordings inside the app, clear the app's storage from Android Settings → Apps → ClarityCam → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. Advertising data is held by Google, not by us — use the Android ad settings above to reset or opt out.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated policy will be posted here with a new date, and the app's Play Data safety section will be updated to match before that version ships.

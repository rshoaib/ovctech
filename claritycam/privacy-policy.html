ClarityCam — Privacy Policy

Published by OVC Tech

Last updated: August 14, 2026

The short version

ClarityCam does not collect anything. Your practice videos, their transcripts and your history stay on your phone. There is no account, no server and no analytics — and the version on Google Play is built without the Android internet permission, so the app cannot send anything anywhere even if we wanted it to.

What the app records, and where it goes

  • Video and audio. When you press record, the app films you for up to 90 seconds using your phone's camera and microphone. The file is written to ClarityCam's own private storage area on the device. It is not added to your gallery, not shared with other apps, and never uploaded.
  • Speech transcript. While you record, your phone's built-in offline speech recognition converts what you say to text so the app can work out your speaking pace and count filler words. This runs entirely on the device. ClarityCam explicitly requests on-device recognition and never falls back to a network recogniser.
  • Your history. Dates, prompts, word counts, pace and filler figures are saved in the app's own local storage so it can show your streak and trend.

All of this lives only on your phone. Uninstalling ClarityCam deletes every recording and every number along with it, and there is no copy anywhere else to recover from.

Backups

Android can normally copy an app's data to a user's Google Drive automatically. ClarityCam turns this off (allowBackup=false), so your recordings and transcripts are not included in a device backup.

Permissions, and why each one exists

  • Camera — to film your practice run. Used only while the recording screen is open.
  • Microphone — to record your voice and, via your phone's offline speech recognition, to measure your pace and filler words.
  • Notifications — only if you switch on the optional daily reminder. These are scheduled locally by your phone; no server is involved.
  • Run at startup — so a reminder you set survives a restart.
  • Keep awake — so the screen does not lock in the middle of a 90-second recording.

The app does not request internet access, location, contacts, or access to your photo library.

Children

ClarityCam is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Third parties

There are none. No advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party accounts. Speech recognition is performed by the software already built into your phone, not by a service ClarityCam sends your audio to.

Changes

If this policy ever changes — for instance if a future version needed network access — the updated policy will be posted here with a new date, and the app's Play Data safety section will be updated to match before that version ships.

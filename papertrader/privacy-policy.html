Paper Trader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Introduction

Paper Trader ("the app") is a personal paper-trading and strategy-research tool for Binance USDⓈ-M perpetual futures, developed by Rizwan Shoaib (OVC Tech). Every trade in the app is simulated with virtual USDT. The app never connects to an exchange account, never asks for exchange API keys or wallet keys, never places real orders and handles no real money. It is distributed only to a closed internal testing group. It has no ads and no analytics, and it is not financial advice.

Data the app stores

  • Anonymous user ID — the app signs in with Firebase Anonymous Authentication, which creates a random user ID. No name, email or phone number is asked for or stored.
  • Your paper-trading records — the virtual account balance, simulated orders, positions, completed trades, the checklist and strategy you tagged each trade with, strategy names and rules you write, journal notes you type, and the reports computed from them. These are stored in our Firebase (Google Cloud Firestore) database under your anonymous user ID so the server can simulate stop-loss, take-profit, limit fills, funding and liquidation while the app is closed.
  • Push notification token — a Firebase Cloud Messaging token, used only to tell you when a strategy reaches 30, 50 or 100 completed paper trades.
  • App settings — the simulated fee and slippage model and your starting balance.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising ID or any other personal data. Please do not type personal information into journal notes.

Third-party services

  • Binance public market data — the app and our server read anonymous, keyless public price, order-book and funding data. No data about you is sent to Binance.
  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, server functions and push delivery (Firebase privacy).
  • Google Play — the app may ask Google Play whether an update is available.

Sharing, security and retention

We do not sell or share any data. All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Only you (through the app) and the app's own server can read your records; other users cannot. Records are kept while you use the app. "Reset account" in the app starts a new paper account. To have all records for your anonymous ID deleted from our server, email us; uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed to children.

Legal Policy Gen — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026 · describes Legal Policy Gen 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Legal Policy Gen collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The business details and questionnaire answers you type are stored only on your phone, and the documents the app assembles from them leave the phone only when you copy or share them yourself.

What the app stores

  • Your business profile (business name, site or app name, web address, contact email, country, and whether the documents cover a website, an app or both) and, for each saved document, its kind, your yes/no answers and the effective date. These are kept in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • When you export a PDF, the file is written to the app's cache folder and handed to the share sheet; the app you choose receives the file. Copying puts the text on the clipboard.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. The app turns off Android cloud backup.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not request any runtime permission: no storage, contacts, location, camera or microphone. Sharing and clipboard access go through the Android share sheet and clipboard APIs, which need no permission.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not ask for your name, email or any account. The contact email you type into a document is yours to publish; the app does nothing with it.

The documents it writes

The privacy policies, terms, cookie policies, disclaimers and refund policies the app produces are templates assembled from your answers. They are not legal advice, and OVC Tech is not a law firm. You are responsible for checking that a document matches what your business really does before publishing it.

Children

The app is a business tool for adults (18 and over) who run a website or an app. It is not designed for children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version ever collects data (for example, if advertising is added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the Google Play data-safety section will say so.

Recipe Cost Calc — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026 · describes Recipe Cost Calc 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Recipe Cost Calc collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The ingredients, prices and recipes you enter stay on your phone, in storage only this app can read.

What the app stores, on your phone only

  • The ingredients you type: a name, what a pack costs, how much is in the pack and its unit, and an optional grams-per-cup figure.
  • Your recipes: a name, how much one batch makes, the ingredient lines with their amounts and units, and the optional waste, overhead, labour and markup figures.
  • The currency code you chose as a label for prices.

All of it lives in the app's private storage on your device. Deleting an ingredient or a recipe removes it immediately; uninstalling the app removes everything. The app turns off Android cloud backup, so your data is not copied to a Google account.

Sharing is always your choice

  • Share cost sheet writes a plain-text cost breakdown and opens your phone's share sheet. You pick the app to send it with, and you can cancel there. Recipe Cost Calc itself sends nothing.
  • Share backup works the same way: all your ingredients and recipes as one text file, handed to the app you choose.
  • Restore reads only the backup text you paste into the app.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not request any other permission: no contacts, camera, microphone, location, files or notifications.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not ask for your name, email or any account.

Children

The app is a general-audience utility aimed at ages 13 and up. It is not designed for children and collects no data from anyone.

Not advice

Recipe Cost Calc does arithmetic on the prices you type. It is not pricing, business, tax or nutrition advice; the suggested selling price is your markup applied to your own figures.

Changes

If a future version ever collects data (for example, if advertising is added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the Google Play data-safety section will say so.

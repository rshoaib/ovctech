Dev Toolbox — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026 · describes Dev Toolbox 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Dev Toolbox collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The text you type or paste is processed on the phone for the result on screen and is not saved.

What the app stores

  • Nothing. The app has no saved history and no settings. When you close it, the text you typed or pasted is gone.
  • When you tap Copy, that one result is placed on the Android clipboard, where other apps you use can read it like anything else you copy. The app never reads the clipboard on its own.
  • The app turns off Android cloud backup; there is no app data to back up in any case.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not request any other permission: no camera, microphone, contacts, location, files or notifications.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not ask for your name, email or any account.

Children

The app is a general-audience developer utility aimed at ages 13 and up. It is not designed for children and collects no data from anyone.

Accuracy and limits

Results are computed with standard algorithms (RFC 4648 Base64, MD5 and the SHA-2 family through the operating system, ECMAScript URL encoding and JSON). The JWT decoder only reads a token and does not verify its signature, so it cannot tell you a token is genuine.

Changes

If a future version ever collects data (for example, if advertising is added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the Google Play data-safety section will say so.

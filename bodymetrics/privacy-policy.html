BMI & Body Metrics Calc — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026 · describes BMI & Body Metrics Calc 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

BMI & Body Metrics Calc collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The measurements you type (height, weight, age, waist, hip, neck) are used for the calculation on screen and are not saved.

What the app stores

  • Nothing. The app has no saved history, no profile and no settings. When you close it, the numbers you typed are gone.
  • The app turns off Android cloud backup; there is no app data to back up in any case.

Health data

Height, weight, age, sex and body measurements are health-related information. The app processes them only in memory on your phone, for the result shown on screen, and never stores, transmits or shares them. It does not connect to Health Connect, Google Fit or any other health platform.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not request any other permission: no camera, microphone, contacts, location, files, body sensors or notifications.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not ask for your name, email or any account.

Children

The app applies formulas derived for adults and is aimed at ages 18 and up. It is not designed for children and collects no data from anyone.

Not medical advice

The app applies published population formulas (BMI, Mifflin-St Jeor, Harris-Benedict, Devine, Robinson, Miller, Hamwi, waist ratios, the US Navy body-fat method) and shows its working. The results are estimates, not a diagnosis, a treatment or a diet plan. Talk to a doctor or dietitian about your own health.

Changes

If a future version ever collects data (for example, if advertising is added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the Google Play data-safety section will say so.

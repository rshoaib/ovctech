Maths Formula Reference — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026 · describes Maths Formula Reference 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Maths Formula Reference collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Every formula, explanation and worked example is built into the app.

What the app stores

  • Nothing. The app has no settings, no favourites and no history. What you type in the search box exists only on screen and is gone when you close the app.
  • The app turns off Android cloud backup; there is no app data to back up in any case.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not request any permission at all: no microphone, camera, contacts, location, storage or notifications.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not ask for your name, email or any account.

Children

The app is a general-audience study reference aimed at ages 13 and up (secondary and university students, teachers). It is not designed for children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version ever collects data (for example, if advertising is added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the Google Play data-safety section will say so.

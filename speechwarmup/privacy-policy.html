Speech Warm-Up — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026 · describes Speech Warm-Up 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Speech Warm-Up collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere, and it does not use the microphone. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.

What the app stores, on your phone only

  • Your warm-up history: for each routine you finish, which routine it was, how long it lasts and when you finished it. This is what the day streak and the "warm-ups done" count are worked out from.
  • Your settings: whether the phone vibrates at each new step and whether the screen stays on during a routine.

All of it lives in the app's private storage on your device. "Clear history" in Settings deletes the history immediately; uninstalling the app removes everything. The app turns off Android cloud backup, so your data is not copied to a Google account.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not record or listen. It requests no microphone, contacts, camera, location, files or notifications permission.
  • Its only permission is VIBRATE, for the buzz at each new step; you can turn the buzz off in Settings. Keeping the screen on during a routine needs no permission and can also be turned off.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.

Children

Speech Warm-Up is a general-audience app for teenagers and adults and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version changes what the app stores or shares, this page will be updated first and the version it describes will change in the line under the title.

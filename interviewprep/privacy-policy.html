Interview Prep: Practice Q&A — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026 · describes Interview Prep 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Interview Prep collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It does not record audio: you speak, the app times you, you rate yourself. Your ratings and notes stay on your phone, in storage only this app can read.

What the app stores, on your phone only

  • For each timed answer: which question it was, the rating you gave yourself (1 to 5), how many seconds the answer took, and when.
  • For each question you write notes under: the text of those notes.

All of it lives in a small database in the app's private storage on your device. “Clear ratings” on the About screen removes every rating and timing; uninstalling the app removes everything, notes included. The app turns off Android cloud backup, so nothing is copied to a Google account.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not request any other permission: no microphone, camera, contacts, location, files or notifications. The answer timer runs from the phone clock and does not listen to you.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not ask for your name, email, employer or any other personal information.

Children

Interview Prep is a general-audience practice app for job seekers and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version changes what the app stores or shares, this page will be updated first and the version it describes will change in the line under the title.

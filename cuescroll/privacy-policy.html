CueScroll Teleprompter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026 · describes CueScroll Teleprompter 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

CueScroll collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Your scripts stay on your phone.

What the app stores

  • The scripts you write (title and text) and your prompter settings (speed, text size, mirror, countdown, reading line), in one file in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Nothing else. Deleting a script removes it from that file; uninstalling the app removes the file.
  • The app turns off Android cloud backup, so this file is not copied to Google Drive.

Sharing a script

If you tap Share as text, Android's share sheet opens and you choose where the text goes (for example, an email or notes app). That app then handles it under its own privacy policy. CueScroll never shares anything on its own.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not record video or sound and requests no permission at all: no camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage or notifications.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not ask for your name, email or any account.

Children

The app is a general-audience productivity tool aimed at ages 13 and up. It is not designed for children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version ever collects data (for example, if advertising is added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the Google Play data-safety section will say so.

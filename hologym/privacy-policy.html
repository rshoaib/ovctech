Hologym — Privacy Policy

Published by OVC Tech

Last updated: July 27, 2026

The short version

Hologym collects nothing. There are no accounts, no servers, no sync, no analytics, and no advertising. The app has no internet permission at all — it is an interactive muscle-map reference that runs entirely on your device. That is the entire point of the app.

How the app works

Hologym shows an interactive holographic body map. You tap a muscle group and the app displays a built-in list of exercises that target it. The muscle map and the exercise list are bundled inside the app; nothing you do is recorded, transmitted, or stored off the device. In fact, the INTERNET permission is removed from the app entirely, so it is technically incapable of sending any information anywhere. There is no backend to send anything to.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ We do NOT collect your personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • ❌ We do NOT collect health, fitness, or body data — you enter nothing
  • ❌ We do NOT track your location
  • ❌ We do NOT use any analytics, trackers, or crash-reporting SDKs
  • ❌ We do NOT serve ads and do NOT use an advertising ID
  • ❌ We do NOT sell or share your data (there is no data to sell or share)
  • ❌ We do NOT require an account or login

Permissions

Hologym requests no runtime permissions. It has no camera, microphone, location, storage, or internet access. It simply displays its built-in content.

No purchases, no ads

Hologym is completely free. There are no in-app purchases and no advertising. It does not contain an advertising SDK and does not request or use an advertising ID.

Data Retention & Deletion

Because the app stores nothing off-device and operates no servers, there is nothing for us to retain, delete, or hand over. Uninstalling the app removes it entirely from your device.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13, and is suitable for general audiences.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always indicated by the "Last updated" date above.

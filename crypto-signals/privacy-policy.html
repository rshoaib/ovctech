📈 Crypto Trading Signals — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Introduction

Crypto Trading Signals ("we", "our", "the app") is a free crypto market-signal reader developed and published by Rizwan Shoaib. It reads public cryptocurrency market data for a fixed list of ten well-known trading pairs and derives trading levels from it using standard technical calculations. It has no accounts, no ads, and collects no personal information.

Unlike a purely on-device app, this one is backed by a small server component (Google Cloud Functions and Firebase Firestore) that reads the same public market data on a schedule so that signals are produced even while the app is closed. This policy explains exactly what that server does and does not hold.

Data We Access

  • Public market data — the app and its server connect to Binance's public, keyless market-data endpoints to read candlestick history and live prices for a fixed list of ten well-known trading pairs (for example BTC/USDT). These are anonymous, read-only public price feeds. No API key, no account, and no personal or device identifier is sent to Binance. The app cannot place orders, cannot access any exchange account, and cannot move funds.
  • Settings you choose — your alert and haptic preferences, the numbers you type into the position-size calculator, which signals you have marked as taken, and the most recent signals shown are stored locally on your device only. None of this is ever transmitted.
  • A notification token — only if you turn alerts on — to send you a push notification when a new signal is published, Android must give us a Firebase Cloud Messaging registration token for your installation. If, and only if, you enable "New-signal alerts" in Settings, that token is sent to our server and stored so a notification can be delivered to your device.

    The token is a random identifier for an app installation. It is not your name, email, phone number, advertising ID, or hardware ID; it changes if you reinstall the app and it cannot be used to identify you personally. Alongside it we store only the platform ("android") and the app version, so a broken build can be recognised. Nothing about which pairs you view, which signals you copy or mark, or how you use the app is ever sent with it.

How Signals Are Produced

Signals are calculated from the public price history described above by applying published, standard technical-analysis calculations — a moving-average trend filter and an Average True Range volatility measure — to derive an entry range, a stop-loss level and take-profit targets. There is no signal provider and no analyst.

The calculation runs on our server, every 15 minutes, so that a signal is produced even when the app is closed. The results are written to a database that the app reads. That database holds only market levels — a pair name, a direction, an entry range, a stop and targets. It contains nothing about you, and it is the same for every user; there is no per-user content of any kind.

If the app cannot reach the server, it falls back to performing the identical calculation on your device.

Local Storage

The app stores the following on your device, using your device's private on-device storage:

  • The most recent signals shown, so the app reopens where you left it
  • Recently closed signals shown in the History tab
  • Your alert and haptic-feedback preferences

This data never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

  • Internet — Required. Used to read Binance's public market-data endpoints and to read published signals from our server. It is not used for advertising, analytics, or transmitting anything about you.
  • Notifications — Optional. Used only to alert you when a new signal is published. Turning this on is what causes a notification token to be sent to our server (see above). Turning it off, or denying the permission, means no token is ever sent.
  • Vibrate — Used only for haptic feedback when you copy a price.

The app does not request or use an advertising ID.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your settings, marked trades and cached signals are stored locally on your device only, and stay there for as long as the app is installed.

The only thing held on our server is the notification token, and only if you enabled alerts. It is kept for as long as it stays valid. When Google tells us a token is dead — which is what happens when you uninstall the app or disable notifications — our server deletes it automatically on the next send.

How to delete your data:

  • Turn off "New-signal alerts" in the app's Settings, or revoke the notification permission in Android settings. The stored token stops being valid and is deleted automatically.
  • Go to your device's Settings → Apps → Crypto Trading Signals → Storage → Clear Data to erase all locally stored app data, or
  • Uninstall the app. This deletes all local data immediately and causes the stored notification token to be deleted automatically.
  • You can also email us at segmentbi@gmail.com to request deletion of a token, and we will remove it.

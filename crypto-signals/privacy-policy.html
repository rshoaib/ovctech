📈 Crypto Trading Signals — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Introduction

Crypto Trading Signals ("we", "our", "the app") is a free crypto market-signal reader developed and published by Rizwan Shoaib. The app reads public cryptocurrency market data and derives trading levels on your device. It has no accounts, no ads, and collects no personal information whatsoever. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do.

Data We Access

  • Public market data — the app connects to Binance's public, keyless market-data endpoints to read candlestick history and live prices for a fixed list of well-known trading pairs (for example BTC/USDT). These are anonymous, read-only public price feeds. No API key, no account, and no personal or device identifier is sent. The app cannot place orders, cannot access any exchange account, and cannot move funds.
  • Settings you choose — your alert and haptic preferences, the numbers you type into the position-size calculator, and the most recent signals shown in the app are stored locally on your device only.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ We do NOT collect your personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • ❌ We do NOT have user accounts, logins, or profiles
  • ❌ We do NOT upload any of your data to any server — we operate no server
  • ❌ We do NOT track your location
  • ❌ We do NOT sell or share your data with anyone
  • ❌ We do NOT serve ads of any kind
  • ❌ We do NOT use an advertising ID
  • ❌ We do NOT use third-party analytics, crash reporting, or trackers
  • ❌ We do NOT ask for, store, or transmit exchange API keys or wallet credentials

How Signals Are Produced

Signals are calculated inside the app on your device from the public price history described above. There is no signal provider, no analyst, and no remote service involved. The app applies published, standard technical-analysis calculations — a moving average trend filter and an Average True Range volatility measure — to derive an entry range, a stop-loss level, and take-profit targets.

Because everything is computed locally, nothing about which pairs you look at, which signals you copy, or how you use the app is ever transmitted anywhere.

Local Storage

The app stores the following on your device, using your device's private on-device storage:

  • The most recent signals shown, so the app reopens where you left it
  • Recently closed signals shown in the History tab
  • Your alert and haptic-feedback preferences

This data never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

  • Internet — Required. Used solely to read Binance's public market-data endpoints so prices and signals are current. It is not used for advertising, analytics, or transmitting anything about you.
  • Notifications — Optional. Used only to alert you on your own device when the app computes a new signal. These notifications are generated and shown entirely on your device; there is no push server and no push token.
  • Vibrate — Used only for haptic feedback when you copy a price.

The app does not request or use an advertising ID.

Data Retention & Deletion

All app data is stored locally on your device only. We operate no servers, so there is no server-side data to retain or delete.

How long we retain data: local data stays on your device for as long as the app is installed. There is no expiration.

How to delete your data:

  • Go to your device's Settings → Apps → Crypto Trading Signals → Storage → Clear Data to erase all locally stored app data, or
  • Uninstall the app, which permanently deletes all associated data from your device.

Not Financial Advice

Crypto Trading Signals is an information tool only. The levels it shows are the output of standard technical calculations applied to public price data — they are not financial, investment, or trading advice, and they are not a recommendation to buy or sell anything. Past price behaviour does not predict future results. Any decision you make is your own, and you should consider seeking advice from a qualified professional.

Our app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app is intended for a general adult audience.

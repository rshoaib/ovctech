TravelDocs — Privacy Policy

Published by Rizwan Shoaib

Last updated: October 2, 2026

The short version

TravelDocs does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no AI services and no network connection. Every document you add stays on your device, encrypted, behind your phone's fingerprint, face or screen-lock PIN. A document leaves the phone only when you yourself choose to share it.

How the app works

TravelDocs is an offline wallet for your own identity documents: ID cards and driving licences (front and back), vehicle registration, passport, utility account, your portrait photos for applications, and any other files such as tickets, bank statements and insurance. You add a document by taking a photo with your phone's camera app, or by choosing an image, a PDF (or one page of it) or another file. The app encrypts it into its own private storage and remembers only which slot or type it belongs to, which side it is, and the date you added it. The original file name is not kept. A destination checklist shows which documents are typically needed for a trip. None of this ever leaves your device. The app does not hold the Internet permission, so it has no way to upload anything.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ We do NOT collect your personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • ❌ We do NOT upload your documents, images, files, or their details anywhere
  • ❌ We do NOT connect to the Internet — the app has no network permission
  • ❌ We do NOT send your documents to any AI or text-recognition (OCR) service
  • ❌ We do NOT track your location, and images are re-saved without location data
  • ❌ We do NOT use any analytics, crash-reporting, trackers, or advertising SDKs
  • ❌ We do NOT serve ads and do NOT use an advertising ID
  • ❌ We do NOT sell or share your data (there is no data to sell or share)
  • ❌ We do NOT require an account or login

Where your documents are stored

  • Encrypted on the device. Each document is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before it is written. The encryption key is created inside your phone's secure key store (Android Keystore) and cannot be exported from it.
  • No copies left behind. Files you pick are read directly from the system picker and encrypted in one step; no unencrypted copy is saved. A camera photo is encrypted as soon as you return from the camera, and the temporary original is deleted.
  • Location stripped. Document images are re-saved as plain JPEGs, which removes the location, camera model and other details a phone photo normally carries. Portrait photos are kept pixel-for-pixel (so they still meet visa photo rules); only their hidden metadata is removed. PDF pages are turned into images on the phone itself, using Android's built-in PDF renderer.
  • No backup, no transfer. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are both disabled for this app, so your documents are never copied to Google Drive, a new phone, or any other cloud by the operating system.
  • Screens protected. Document screens block screenshots and screen recording, and are blanked in the recent-apps switcher.
  • Deleting. When you delete a document, the encrypted file and its record are removed.

Sharing a document

You can send one document at a time to someone else. TravelDocs never creates links or uploads anything for this: it asks you to confirm, then hands a single copy to your phone's standard share menu, and you choose the app and the recipient. You can stamp the copy with a watermark such as "for car rental only" and the date, so it is harder to reuse for anything else. The copy is made available to the app you pick through a temporary, single-use permission. When you come back to TravelDocs that permission is withdrawn and the copy is deleted. Once you have sent a document, what the receiving app and the recipient do with it is governed by their own privacy practices, not this policy.

Files that TravelDocs cannot display itself, such as PDFs, are opened in a viewer app the same way: a temporary copy is handed over and deleted when you return.

The "Apply on official site" button

On a destination's checklist, an Apply on official site button can open the official government or visa-application website for that destination in your phone's normal web browser. It hands off to your browser — a separate app — rather than TravelDocs connecting to a network itself. TravelDocs never sends any of your personal data or documents to those sites; it simply opens the public page. What happens once you are in your browser is governed by that website's and your browser's own privacy policies.

Permissions

  • Biometrics (fingerprint / face) — Used only to lock and unlock the app on your own device. Your fingerprint and face data are handled entirely by Android's secure system; the app never sees or stores them and only receives a "success / failure" result.
  • Camera — TravelDocs does not hold the camera permission. "Take photo" opens your phone's own camera app, which hands the single photo back to TravelDocs.
  • No Internet permission — The app deliberately does not hold the Internet permission, so it cannot transmit data.

Security

TravelDocs only opens behind your phone's own lock: fingerprint or face, or your screen-lock PIN, pattern or password. If your phone has no screen lock at all, the app will not open your documents until you set one. It locks again whenever you leave it, except when you come straight back (within two minutes) from the camera, file picker or share menu that you opened from inside the app. No method is perfectly secure, and anyone who knows your phone's PIN can unlock the app, so keep a strong screen lock on your phone.

Data Retention & Deletion

All data lives on your device only. Because we operate no servers, there is nothing for us to retain, delete, or hand over.

  • Delete individual documents from within the app, or
  • Go to Settings → Apps → TravelDocs → Storage → Clear Data to erase all locally stored app data, or
  • Uninstall the app to permanently remove everything associated with it from your device.

Children's Privacy

TravelDocs is a personal document tool intended for adults. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data at all.

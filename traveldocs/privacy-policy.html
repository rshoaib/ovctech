TravelDocs — Privacy Policy

Published by Rizwan Shoaib

Last updated: July 19, 2026

The short version

TravelDocs does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no network connection. Every document you add stays on your device, in the app's own private storage, protected behind your device's fingerprint or face unlock. That is the entire point of the app.

How the app works

TravelDocs is an offline vault for your own travel documents (passport, residency/ID card, photos, tickets, bank statements, insurance, and so on). When you add a document, the app copies the file you pick into its own private, on-device storage and remembers a little information about it (the file name, which document type you chose, and the date you added it). It also lets you pick a destination and see a guidance checklist of documents that are typically needed. None of this ever leaves your device. The app does not request Internet access — it cannot upload anything, because it has no way to reach a network.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ We do NOT collect your personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • ❌ We do NOT upload your documents, files, or their details anywhere
  • ❌ We do NOT connect to the Internet — the app has no network permission
  • ❌ We do NOT track your location
  • ❌ We do NOT use any analytics, trackers, or advertising SDKs
  • ❌ We do NOT serve ads and do NOT use an advertising ID
  • ❌ We do NOT sell or share your data (there is no data to sell or share)
  • ❌ We do NOT require an account or login

Where your documents are stored

Documents you add are copied into the app's own private, on-device storage — an area that other apps cannot read. The app does not browse the rest of your files. When you tap Open on a document, your device's normal "open with / share" sheet appears so you can view it in another app of your choice; TravelDocs itself sends nothing anywhere. When you Delete a document, the file is removed from disk and its record is erased. Automatic cloud backup of the app is disabled (allowBackup=false), so your documents are never synced to Google Drive or any other cloud by the operating system.

The "Apply on official site" button

On a destination's checklist, an Apply on official site button can open the official government or visa-application website for that destination in your phone's normal web browser. This is the only feature that touches the Internet, and it does so by handing off to your browser — a separate app — not by the TravelDocs app connecting to a network itself. TravelDocs never sends any of your personal data or documents to those sites; it simply opens the public page. What happens once you are in your browser is governed by that website's and your browser's own privacy policies.

Permissions

  • Biometrics (fingerprint / face) — Used only to lock and unlock the app on your own device. Your fingerprint and face data are handled entirely by Android's secure system; the app never sees or stores them and only receives a "success / failure" result.
  • No Internet permission — The app deliberately does not hold the Internet permission, so it cannot transmit data.

Security

Access to the app is protected by your device's own biometric or device-credential lock. If no biometric is enrolled, the app tells you it cannot lock itself and encourages you to set up a device screen lock. Because your documents can be sensitive (for example, a passport), keeping a secure lock on your phone is strongly recommended.

Data Retention & Deletion

All data lives on your device only. Because we operate no servers, there is nothing for us to retain, delete, or hand over.

  • Delete individual documents from within the app, or
  • Go to Settings → Apps → TravelDocs → Storage → Clear Data to erase all locally stored app data, or
  • Uninstall the app to permanently remove everything associated with it from your device.

Children's Privacy

TravelDocs is a personal document tool intended for adults. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data at all.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always indicated by the "Last updated" date above.

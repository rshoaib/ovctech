Split Tab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026 · describes Split Tab 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Split Tab collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The groups, names and amounts you enter stay on your phone, in storage only this app can read.

What the app stores, on your phone only

  • Your groups: a name, a currency code used as a label, and the names you type for the people in the group. Names are typed by you; the app does not read your contacts.
  • Your entries: what an expense was, the amount, the date, who paid, how it is split, an optional note, and the payments you record between people.

All of it lives in the app's private storage on your device. Deleting an entry or a group removes it immediately; uninstalling the app removes everything. The app turns off Android cloud backup, so your data is not copied to a Google account.

Sharing is always your choice

  • Share summary writes a plain-text summary of a group (total, balances, the settle-up plan and the entries) and opens your phone's share sheet. You pick the app to send it with, and you can cancel there. Split Tab itself sends nothing.
  • Share backup works the same way: all your groups and entries as one text file, handed to the app you choose.
  • Restore reads only the backup text you paste into the app.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not request any other permission: no contacts, camera, microphone, location, files or notifications.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not move money. It does arithmetic on the amounts you type and is not financial advice.

Children

Split Tab is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version changes what the app stores or shares, this page will be updated first and the version it describes will change in the line under the title.

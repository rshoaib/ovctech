Expiro — Privacy Policy

Published by Rizwan Shoaib

Last updated: October 2, 2026

The short version

Every asset you track, every reminder and every document photo you add stays on your device. There are no accounts, no servers of ours and no sync. From version 1.1.0 Expiro is free and supported by a single banner ad served by Google AdMob; to request it the app needs internet access and uses your device's advertising ID. Nothing you enter ever leaves your phone. What does leave it is the ad request, plus the usage metrics Google's on-device text scanner reports about itself; this policy explains exactly what both involve. Earlier versions carried no ads and had no internet permission at all.

How the app works

Expiro helps you track things that expire (registrations, warranties, insurance, subscriptions) or wear out with use (service intervals and other usage limits), and lets you attach photos of the related documents. Everything you enter — asset names, dates, readings, notes and document images — is stored locally on your device in the app's own private on-device database and file storage. Nothing you enter is transmitted off your device; there is no backend to send it to. The app's only network activity is requesting ads from Google AdMob (see Advertising below) and the usage metrics described under Document scanning below.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ We do NOT collect your personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • ❌ We do NOT upload your assets, dates, readings, notes, or document photos anywhere
  • ❌ We do NOT collect your location from your device's location services
  • ❌ We do NOT run analytics of our own on anything you enter, and there is no crash-reporting SDK
  • ❌ We do NOT sell your personal data
  • ❌ We do NOT require an account or login

Advertising

From version 1.1.0, Expiro is free and supported by ads. It displays a single banner ad served by Google AdMob at the bottom of the Assets tab. There is no full-screen ad, no ad on launch, and no ad on the Alerts tab or on an asset's own page.

To request an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app sends the following to Google and its advertising partners:

  • Your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier Android provides for advertising) and app-set ID.
  • Your IP address, from which Google derives an approximate location. Expiro never asks for location permission.
  • Coarse device and app information — device model, OS version, app version, and interaction with the ad itself.
  • Diagnostics about the ad request, such as whether it loaded.

This data is collected by Google and shared with Google as an advertising partner. It is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google's handling of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the AdMob partner policies.

Consent in Europe. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Expiro shows a Google-certified consent form the first time it runs and records your choice. If you do not consent to personalised advertising, the app requests non-personalised ads instead. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Ad content is limited to Google's "G" (general audience) rating.

Document scanning

"Scan from photo" reads the text on a document image using Google ML Kit text recognition. The recognition model is bundled inside the app and runs entirely on your device: the image and the text read from it are never sent anywhere.

As Google documents in its ML Kit data disclosure, ML Kit itself may send Google information about how the feature is performing: device information (manufacturer, model, OS version), app package name and version, a per-installation identifier that is not intended to identify you, performance metrics such as latency, API configuration, and error codes. Google uses this for diagnostics and usage analytics, encrypts it in transit, and does not transfer it to third parties. It contains none of your images, text or assets.

Where your data is stored

Your assets and their details are saved in the app's private on-device database. Document photos you attach are copied into the app's own private storage area on your device; only a local reference to each image is kept. None of it is transmitted off the device at any point — the ad request described above carries no asset, date, note or photo. If you delete an asset or a document inside the app, the corresponding data and image file are removed from your device.

Permissions

  • Notifications — Optional. For date-based assets you can ask Expiro to remind you a chosen number of days before an expiry date. The reminder is scheduled and delivered entirely on your device; nothing is sent to or from a server. You can turn reminders off at any time in your device settings.
  • Camera & Photos — Optional. Used only if you choose to photograph or import a document image to attach to an asset. Images are stored on your device only and are never uploaded.
  • Run at startup — Optional. Used only so your local reminders can be re-established after a device restart. It performs no background network activity.
  • Internet — Used to request ads, and by ML Kit for the usage metrics described above. Your assets, notes and photos are never sent over it.
  • Advertising ID — Used by Google AdMob to serve ads, as described above.

No purchases

Expiro is completely free. There are no in-app purchases. The app is supported by the banner ad described above.

Data Retention & Deletion

All of your data lives on your device only. Because we operate no servers, there is nothing for us to retain, delete, or hand over. To remove your data you can:

  • Delete individual assets or documents from within the app, or
  • Go to Settings → Apps → Expiro → Storage → Clear Data to erase all locally stored app data, or
  • Uninstall the app to permanently remove everything associated with it from your device.

Advertising data is held by Google, not by us — use the Android ad settings above to reset or opt out.

Children's Privacy

Expiro is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always indicated by the "Last updated" date above, and the app's Play Data safety section is updated to match before a version that changes what the app does ships.

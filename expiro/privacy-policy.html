Expiro — Privacy Policy

Published by OVC Tech

Last updated: July 25, 2026

The short version

Expiro collects nothing. There are no accounts, no servers, no sync, no analytics, and no advertising. The app has no internet permission at all — every asset you track, every reminder, and every document photo you add stays on your device. That is the entire point of the app.

How the app works

Expiro helps you track things that expire (registrations, warranties, insurance, subscriptions) or wear out with use (service intervals and other usage limits), and lets you attach photos of the related documents. Everything you enter — asset names, dates, readings, notes and document images — is stored locally on your device in the app's own private on-device database and file storage. The app performs no network requests and transmits no data off your device. In fact, the INTERNET permission is removed from the app entirely, so it is technically incapable of sending your data anywhere. There is no backend to send anything to.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ We do NOT collect your personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • ❌ We do NOT upload your assets, dates, readings, notes, or document photos anywhere
  • ❌ We do NOT track your location
  • ❌ We do NOT use any analytics, trackers, or crash-reporting SDKs
  • ❌ We do NOT serve ads and do NOT use an advertising ID
  • ❌ We do NOT sell or share your data (there is no data to sell or share)
  • ❌ We do NOT require an account or login

Where your data is stored

Your assets and their details are saved in the app's private on-device database. Document photos you attach are copied into the app's own private storage area on your device; only a local reference to each image is kept. Nothing is transmitted off the device at any point. If you delete an asset or a document inside the app, the corresponding data and image file are removed from your device.

Permissions

  • Notifications — Optional. For date-based assets you can ask Expiro to remind you a chosen number of days before an expiry date. The reminder is scheduled and delivered entirely on your device; nothing is sent to or from a server. You can turn reminders off at any time in your device settings.
  • Camera & Photos — Optional. Used only if you choose to photograph or import a document image to attach to an asset. Images are stored on your device only and are never uploaded.
  • Run at startup — Optional. Used only so your local reminders can be re-established after a device restart. It performs no background network activity.

No purchases, no ads

Expiro is completely free. There are no in-app purchases and no advertising. It does not contain an advertising SDK and does not request or use an advertising ID.

Data Retention & Deletion

All data lives on your device only. Because we operate no servers, there is nothing for us to retain, delete, or hand over. To remove your data you can:

  • Delete individual assets or documents from within the app, or
  • Go to Settings → Apps → Expiro → Storage → Clear Data to erase all locally stored app data, or
  • Uninstall the app to permanently remove everything associated with it from your device.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13, and is suitable for general audiences.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always indicated by the "Last updated" date above.

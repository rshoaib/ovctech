Asma ul Husna — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026 · describes Asma ul Husna 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Asma ul Husna collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Your learning progress stays on your phone, in storage only this app can read.

What the app stores, on your phone only

  • For each of the 99 names: whether you marked it as known, whether you starred it as a favourite, and how many quiz answers about it were right or wrong.

All of it lives in a small database in the app's private storage on your device. “Clear known marks” on the About screen resets it; uninstalling the app removes everything. The app turns off Android cloud backup, so your progress is not copied to a Google account.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not request any other permission: no contacts, camera, microphone, location, files or notifications.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not ask for your name, email or any other personal information.

Children

Asma ul Husna is a general-audience learning app and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version changes what the app stores or shares, this page will be updated first and the version it describes will change in the line under the title.

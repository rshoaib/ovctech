Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Your Privacy Matters

Hala, published by Rizwan Shoaib, is built with a privacy-first approach. All your learning data stays on your device. Two optional features use AI services: Conversation practice (the messages you type) and, for Hala Premium subscribers, the Voice tutor (your voice during a lesson). Both are described below.

Data Storage

  • All lesson progress, saved phrases, streaks, and preferences are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage.
  • Your learning data is never sent to our servers. Our only server is the AI relay (a Google Cloud Function) described under Conversation practice and Voice Tutor; it receives only what those features send and never stores your messages or your voice.
  • No user account or registration is required.
We never sell your data. Your learning progress is never transmitted. What does leave your device: limited device data collected by our advertising partner, Google AdMob (see “Advertising”), the messages you choose to type in Conversation practice, and — only if you subscribe to Hala Premium and open a voice lesson — your voice and your subscription status (see below).

Conversation Practice (AI)

Conversation practice lets you chat with an AI tutor in Emirati Arabic. It is optional and only runs when you open it and pick a scenario.

  • What is sent: the scenario you picked, your level, the messages you type in the chat and the last few lines of the conversation (so the tutor can reply in context). Nothing else — no name, no account, no learning history.
  • How it travels: through a relay server we operate (a Google Cloud Function) that holds our AI credentials so the app doesn't have to. The relay forwards your messages in memory and does not store or log them. To keep the free feature fair, it counts messages per installation against a random identifier created on your device (not a device or advertising ID), and allows 40 messages a day.
  • Who processes it: Anthropic (the Claude model) writes the tutor's replies. If Anthropic is unavailable, the same request goes to Google Gemini instead. Only one of them answers each message.
  • Anthropic does not use API content to train its models and deletes it within 30 days; content flagged as violating its usage policies may be kept for up to 2 years for safety review — see Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gemini is used under Google's paid API terms: your content is not used to train Google's models or reviewed by its staff; Google may keep it for a limited period solely to detect abuse — see Google's Privacy Policy.
  • The chat itself is not saved by the app; it disappears when you leave the screen.
Please don't type personal details (full name, phone number, address) in the chat.

Voice Tutor (Hala Premium)

Hala Premium is an optional monthly subscription that adds a live AI voice tutor and practice drills. Nothing below happens unless you subscribe and open a voice lesson or drill.

  • What is sent: while a voice lesson is open, the sound from your microphone, plus the dialect, explanation language, tutor voice and level you picked. In a practice drill, the short recording of you repeating a phrase. No name, no account, no learning history.
  • How it travels: for a live lesson, our server (a Google Cloud Function) gives the app a short-lived key, and the app then streams your voice directly to OpenAI over an encrypted connection. A drill recording goes through our server to OpenAI for transcription. Our server never records or stores your voice.
  • Who processes it: OpenAI hears your voice and writes and speaks the tutor's replies. OpenAI does not use API content to train its models and keeps it for up to 30 days to detect abuse — see OpenAI's Privacy Policy.
  • What we keep: a random identifier created on your device (not a device or advertising ID), the minutes you used in each billing period, the length and settings of each lesson, and the optional 👍/👎 rating and note you choose to send. We keep no audio and no transcript of your lessons. Session records, including any note you send, are deleted after 30 days. A text-free summary of each lesson (length, settings, rating) and your monthly minute totals are kept to run the service and enforce the 150-minute allowance, and are deleted on request.
  • Microphone: used only while a voice lesson or drill is open on screen. Hala never listens in the background.

Voice lessons are AI-generated and can contain mistakes. Please don't say personal details (full name, phone number, address) during a lesson.

Subscriptions & Payments

  • Hala Premium is billed monthly by Google Play until you cancel. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play — we never see your card or bank details.
  • To check your subscription, the app and our server use RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives the Google Play purchase receipt and the random identifier described above, and tells the app and our server whether the subscription is active. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • You can cancel at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Premium stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Advertising

Hala is free and supported by ads. We use Google AdMob to display banner and occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads.

  • To serve ads, Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (including an advertising ID) and usage data, as described in Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland we show a consent prompt (Google’s User Messaging Platform) before any personalized ads are requested. You can decline personalized ads there.
  • You can also reset or opt out of your advertising ID at any time from your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • We do not share your learning progress or any personal information you enter with advertisers.

Analytics & Crash Reporting

Besides advertising, the app uses the following Google services so we can keep it working and improve it:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — usage analytics showing which features are used and whether the app is healthy. It collects an app-instance identifier and the advertising ID, app-interaction events (screens opened, sessions), coarse device information, and an approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reporting. It collects stack traces, the app state at the moment of a crash, device metadata and a Crashlytics installation identifier, so we can find and fix faults.
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring — performance metrics such as start-up time and network latency, plus device metadata and an IP-derived country.

Google acts as our service provider for these. The data is used to run and improve the app; it is not sold, and it is not shared with advertisers. It is separate from the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Notifications — Used only for daily practice reminders you choose to enable.
  • Vibration — Used for haptic feedback on correct answers and milestones.
  • Internet — Required to load ads from Google AdMob. The app's learning content works without a network connection.
  • Microphone — Hala Premium only: used while a voice lesson or practice drill is open, so the tutor can hear you. You can deny or revoke it at any time in Android settings; everything else in Hala keeps working.
  • Google Play billing — Used to buy and restore the optional Hala Premium subscription.

Data Deletion

You can reset all your learning data at any time from the app's settings. Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored data. To ask for deletion of Conversation practice messages processed by Anthropic or Google, or of the voice tutor records described above, contact us with the Support ID shown on the app's Contact screen and we will delete them.

Children's Privacy

Hala is suitable for general audiences but is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can address it.

Changes to This Policy

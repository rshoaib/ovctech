Gold Trade Check — Privacy Policy

Published by OVC Tech

Last updated: August 5, 2026

The short version

Gold Trade Check collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of ours. The app does use the internet, but only to read public market data from Binance's public API — the same numbers anyone can see on a public price chart. Nothing about you is sent anywhere.

Not financial advice. Gold Trade Check is a personal analysis tool. It displays technical indicators and a calculated read of public market data. It does not place trades, hold funds, execute orders, or provide financial, investment, or trading advice. Any decision you make is your own.

How the app works

The app fetches publicly available gold market data — the PAXG/USDT market, where one PAXG token represents one troy ounce of allocated gold — including prices, candles, order book, funding rates, open interest and aggregate positioning statistics, from Binance's public market-data endpoints, runs a set of transparent technical checks on your device, and shows you the result. All calculation happens locally on your phone. We operate no backend, so there is nowhere for your information to be collected even in principle.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ We do NOT collect your personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • ❌ We do NOT require an account, login, or registration
  • ❌ We do NOT track your location
  • ❌ We do NOT use analytics, trackers, or crash-reporting SDKs
  • ❌ We do NOT serve ads and do NOT use an advertising ID
  • ❌ We do NOT sell or share your data (there is no data to sell or share)
  • ❌ We do NOT operate any server that receives data from the app

Network use

The app makes read-only requests to Binance's public market-data API (api.binance.com and fapi.binance.com). These requests ask for market information only — they contain no personal data, no identifiers, and no account information. As with any internet request, Binance will see the connection itself (including your IP address) as their own service logs it; their handling of that is covered by Binance's privacy policy. We receive none of it.

Optional on-device storage

  • Verdict journal — Each run is logged locally so you can review past readings. It stays in the app's private storage on your device and is never uploaded.
  • Trade-setup settings — Values you type in (account balance, risk %, reward:risk, leverage) are stored locally on your device only, purely so the app can calculate reference position sizes. They are never transmitted.
  • Optional Binance API key — Entirely optional and off by default. If you choose to add a read-only Binance API key to see your own position alongside the market read, it is stored encrypted on your device using the Android Keystore (via expo-secure-store). It never leaves your device except inside the signature of a request sent directly to Binance. We never see it, and we recommend using a view-only key with no trade or withdraw permission. You can remove it at any time from within the app.

Permissions

  • Internet — Required. Used only to read public Binance market data.
  • Notifications — Optional. If you enable alerts, the app can notify you when the market read changes. Alerts are evaluated and delivered entirely on your device; no push server is involved.

No purchases, no ads

The app is free. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no advertising. It contains no advertising SDK and does not request or use an advertising ID.

Data Retention & Deletion

All app data lives on your device only. Because we operate no servers, there is nothing for us to retain, delete, or hand over. To remove your data you can:

  • Clear the journal or remove the stored API key from within the app, or
  • Go to Settings → Apps → Gold Trade Check → Storage → Clear Data, or
  • Uninstall the app to permanently remove everything associated with it from your device.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13. It is a market-analysis utility intended for adults and is not directed at children.

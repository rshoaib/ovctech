Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Your Privacy Matters

Mentalism Mastery, published by OVC Tech, is designed with your privacy as a priority. Your learning content stays on your device. The app is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob, described below.

Data Storage

  • All your lesson progress, notes, favorites, and preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • No user account or registration is required.
  • We never sell your personal learning data. The one exception is our advertising partner, Google AdMob, which collects limited device data to show ads — see “Advertising” below.

Advertising

Mentalism Mastery is free and supported by ads. We use Google AdMob to display banner and occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads.

  • To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (including an advertising ID) and usage data, as described in Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising policies.
  • In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland we show a consent prompt (Google’s User Messaging Platform) before any personalized ads are requested. You can decline personalized ads there.
  • You can also reset or opt out of your advertising ID at any time from your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • We do not share your lesson progress, notes, or any personal information you enter into the app with advertisers.
This advertising data is collected and processed by Google as an independent advertising provider, not by us.

Permissions

  • Notifications — Used only for practice reminders you choose to enable.
  • Vibration — Used for haptic feedback on interactions.
  • Internet — Used to load content updates and to load ads from Google AdMob.

Data Deletion

You can reset all locally stored data at any time from the app's settings. Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored data. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time from your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). If you have questions about advertising data, contact us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

Mentalism Mastery is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can address it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at segmentbi@gmail.com.

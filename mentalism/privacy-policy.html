Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Your Privacy Matters

Mentalism Mastery, published by Rizwan Shoaib, is designed with your privacy as a priority. Your learning content stays on your device. The app is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob, described below. One optional drill, “Read This Person”, asks an AI service to write practice scenes; what it sends is described below, and it never includes anything you type or choose.

Data Storage

  • All your lesson progress, notes, favorites, and preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • No user account or registration is required.
  • We never sell your personal learning data. The one exception is our advertising partner, Google AdMob, which collects limited device data to show ads — see “Advertising” below.

Advertising

Mentalism Mastery is free and supported by ads. We use Google AdMob to display banner and occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads.

  • To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (including an advertising ID) and usage data, as described in Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising policies.
  • In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland we show a consent prompt (Google’s User Messaging Platform) before any personalized ads are requested. You can decline personalized ads there.
  • You can also reset or opt out of your advertising ID at any time from your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • We do not share your lesson progress, notes, or any personal information you enter into the app with advertisers.
This advertising data is collected and processed by Google as an independent advertising provider, not by us.

Read This Person (AI-written scenes)

“Read This Person” is an optional drill in the Practice tab. Each time you ask for a scene, an AI model writes a short realistic situation with a person’s observable behaviour, four possible readings, and an explanation of every cue. It runs only when you open the drill and tap the button.

  • What is sent: the difficulty you picked, the app’s language, the titles of the last few scenes you were shown (so the next one is different), and a random identifier created on your device for the daily limit. That identifier is not a device or advertising ID and is not linked to you. Nothing else — there is nothing to type in this drill, and your answer never leaves your device: the whole scene, including the correct reading and the explanation, arrives at once and the app hides them until you have chosen.
  • How it travels: through a relay server we operate (a Google Cloud Function in the EU) that holds our AI credentials so the app does not have to. The relay forwards the request in memory and does not store the scene or log its content; it records only counts, timing and cost. The drill allows 8 scenes a day per installation.
  • Who writes the scenes: Anthropic (the Claude model). If Anthropic is unavailable, the same request goes to Google Gemini instead. Only one of them answers each request, and both act as our service providers under their API terms: requests are not used to train their models. Anthropic may keep API inputs and outputs for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring; Google’s paid API terms apply to Gemini.
  • Offline: without a connection the drill is unavailable and the app offers its built-in drills instead; nothing is queued or sent later.

Analytics & Crash Reporting

Besides advertising, the app uses the following Google services so we can keep it working and improve it:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — usage analytics showing which features are used and whether the app is healthy. It collects an app-instance identifier and the advertising ID, app-interaction events (screens opened, sessions), coarse device information, and an approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reporting. It collects stack traces, the app state at the moment of a crash, device metadata and a Crashlytics installation identifier, so we can find and fix faults.
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring — performance metrics such as start-up time and network latency, plus device metadata and an IP-derived country.

Google acts as our service provider for these. The data is used to run and improve the app; it is not sold, and it is not shared with advertisers. It is separate from the advertising data described above.

Permissions

  • Notifications — Used only for practice reminders you choose to enable.
  • Vibration — Used for haptic feedback on interactions.
  • Internet — Used to load ads from Google AdMob, to send analytics and crash reports, and to fetch AI-written scenes for the “Read This Person” drill.

Data Deletion

You can reset all locally stored data at any time from the app's settings. Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored data. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time from your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). The “Read This Person” drill stores nothing about you on our side; the random daily-limit identifier is removed with the app’s data. If you have questions about advertising data or about requests processed by Anthropic or Google, contact us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

Mentalism Mastery is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can address it.

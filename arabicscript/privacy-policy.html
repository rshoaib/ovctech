Arabic Script Trainer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026 · describes Arabic Script Trainer 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Arabic Script Trainer collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Every lesson, word and drill is built into the app.

What the app stores

  • Nothing. Your answers and scores exist only on screen during a drill and are gone when you close the app. There is no saved history and no settings.
  • The app turns off Android cloud backup; there is no app data to back up in any case.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not request any permission at all: no microphone, camera, contacts, location, storage or notifications.
  • It does not record or listen to anything. There is no audio in this version at all.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not ask for your name, email or any account.

Children

The app is a general-audience language-learning tool aimed at ages 13 and up (adult learners, students, people living or travelling in Arabic-speaking countries). It is not designed for children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version ever collects data (for example, if advertising is added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the Google Play data-safety section will say so.

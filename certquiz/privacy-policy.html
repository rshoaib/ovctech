CertQuiz — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026 · describes CertQuiz 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

CertQuiz collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Your answers and scores are stored on your phone and never leave it.

What the app stores

  • For each question you answer: the question number, its exam domain, whether you got it right, and the time. This powers your per-domain accuracy and the Review missed list.
  • For each finished round: the kind of round (mock exam, domain practice or review), the score and the time.
  • Acronym flashcard progress is not saved; it lasts only while the screen is open.
  • Everything is kept in a database in the app's private storage on this phone. No other app can read it, and it is not backed up to any cloud by the app.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions. It does not request internet, storage, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar or location access.

Deleting your data

Use Reset progress on the About screen. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Apps → CertQuiz → Storage → Clear storage, removes everything the app ever stored. Because nothing leaves your phone, there is nothing for us to delete.

Children

The app is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, children included, and has no ads, links to other apps or ways to contact other people.

IOU Book — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026 · describes IOU Book 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

IOU Book collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The people, amounts and notes you record stay on your phone, in storage only this app can read.

What the app stores, on your phone only

  • The names you type for the people you lend to or borrow from, and a currency code for each.
  • Each entry: its kind (lent, borrowed, paid back), amount, date and optional note.
  • Your most recently used currency, as the default for the next person.

All of it lives in the app's private storage on your device. Deleting a person or an entry removes it immediately; uninstalling the app removes everything. The app turns off Android cloud backup, so the book is not copied to a Google account.

Sharing is always your choice

  • Send reminder writes a short message and opens your phone's share sheet. You pick the app to send it with, and you can edit or cancel it there. IOU Book itself sends nothing.
  • Export as CSV works the same way: the text is handed to the app you choose.

The app does not read your contacts; names are whatever you type.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not request any other permission: no contacts, camera, microphone, location, files or notifications.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not ask for your name, email or any account.

Children

The app is a general-audience utility aimed at ages 13 and up. It is not designed for children and collects no data from anyone.

Not advice

IOU Book is a record of what was lent and borrowed between you and the people you know. It is not financial, tax or legal advice, and it does not enforce or collect anything.

Changes

If a future version ever collects data (for example, if advertising is added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the Google Play data-safety section will say so.

Face Cues — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026 · describes Face Cues 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Face Cues collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It does not use the camera and never sees a photo or video of anyone: every face in the app is a drawing generated by the app itself.

What the app stores

  • Nothing. Your answers and scores exist only on screen during a practice round and are gone when you close the app. There is no saved history and no settings.
  • The app turns off Android cloud backup; there is no app data to back up in any case.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not request any permission at all: no camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage or notifications.
  • It does not analyse faces, photos or video. The faces you practise on are drawn by the app from a fixed set of features.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not ask for your name, email or any account.

Children

The app is a general-audience learning tool aimed at ages 13 and up (students of psychology and communication, speakers, anyone who wants to read faces better). It is not designed for children and collects no data from anyone.

Not an assessment

The app teaches you to recognise drawn expressions. It is a practice tool, not a lie detector and not a psychological, medical or any other assessment of you or of anyone else.

Changes

If a future version ever collects data (for example, if advertising is added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the Google Play data-safety section will say so.

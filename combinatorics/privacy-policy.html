Combinatorics Practice — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026 · describes Combinatorics Practice 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Combinatorics Practice collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.

What the app stores

  • Nothing. The numbers you type into the calculator and your practice scores live only on screen and are gone when you close the app.
  • The app turns off Android cloud backup; there is no data to back up.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It requests no permission at all: no camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage or notifications.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not ask for your name, email or any account.

Children

The app is an education tool for students, teachers and adults, aimed at ages 13 and up. It is not designed for children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version ever collects data (for example, if advertising is added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the Google Play data-safety section will say so.

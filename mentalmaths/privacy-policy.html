Mental Maths Techniques — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026 · describes Mental Maths Techniques 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Mental Maths Techniques collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Your drill results stay in a small database on your phone.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Each drill answer: which technique, whether it was right, and how long it took.
  • The review schedule per technique: how many drills in a row you have passed, the current review interval and the date it is next due.
  • Nothing else. There is no profile, no name and no settings. The numbers you type are compared with the answer and are not kept beyond the right/wrong result above.

All of it is stored only on this phone, in the app's private storage. You can delete it at any time with Reset progress on the About screen, and uninstalling the app removes it. The app turns off Android cloud backup, so this data is not copied to Google's servers by the system either.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not request any other permission: no camera, microphone, contacts, location, files, body sensors or notifications.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not ask for your name, email or any account.

Children

The app is a maths study tool aimed at ages 13 and up (students, teachers and adults). It is not designed for or directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version ever collects data (for example, if advertising is added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the Google Play data-safety section will say so.

Invoice PDF Maker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026 · describes Invoice PDF Maker 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Invoice PDF Maker collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Your business details, your clients and every document you make stay on your phone, in storage only this app can read.

What the app stores, on your phone only

  • Your business profile: the name, address, email, phone, tax ID, currency, payment details and footer line you type, so they can be printed on your documents.
  • Each document: its type, number, dates, the client name, address and email you type, the line items, discount, tax and notes.
  • The most recent PDF for each document, in the app's private cache, until it is replaced or the system clears the cache.

All of it lives in the app's private storage on your device. Deleting a document removes it immediately; uninstalling the app removes everything. The app turns off Android cloud backup, so nothing is copied to a Google account.

Sharing is always your choice

Share PDF builds the PDF on your phone and opens the system share sheet. You pick the app it goes to (email, a chat app, a cloud folder, downloads) and you can cancel there. Invoice PDF Maker itself sends nothing and does not know where a PDF went. What happens to a PDF after you share it is governed by the app you shared it with.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not request any other permission: no contacts, camera, microphone, location, files or notifications. Client details are whatever you type; the app cannot read your contacts.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not ask for your name, email or any account, and it does not process payments.

Children

The app is a business tool intended for adults and older teens (13+). It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version ever collects data (for example, if ads are added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the version it describes is stated at the top.

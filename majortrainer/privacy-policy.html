Major System Trainer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026 · describes Major System Trainer 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Major System Trainer collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Your scores are stored on your phone and never leave it.

What the app stores

  • Your best score in the Sounds and Peg words drills, the longest number you have recalled perfectly in the Listen and Look drills, the length the next recall round starts at, and a count of rounds played. Nothing else.
  • The numbers you are tested on are generated at random on the phone and are not kept after the round.
  • Everything is kept in the app's private storage on this phone. No other app can read it, and it is not backed up to any cloud by the app.

Text-to-speech

The Listen drill reads digits aloud with your phone's own text-to-speech engine (for example Speech Services by Google), chosen in your phone's settings. The app passes it only the digits to read. The app does not use the microphone and records nothing.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions. It does not request internet, storage, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar or location access.

Deleting your data

Use Clear progress on the Reference screen. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Apps → Major System Trainer → Storage → Clear storage, removes everything the app ever stored. Because nothing leaves your phone, there is nothing for us to delete.

Children

The app is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, children included, and has no ads, links to other apps or ways to contact other people.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above (for example by adding advertising), this page will say so before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

Coin Council — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Introduction

Coin Council ("the app", package tech.ovc.coincouncil) is a personal crypto research tool developed by Rizwan Shoaib (OVC Tech). A panel of AI models reviews public market data for liquid cryptocurrency pairs, and the app shows a trade idea only when several models agree. It is distributed only to a closed internal testing group. It has no user accounts, no ads and no analytics. It never places trades and never asks for exchange or wallet keys. It is not financial advice.

Data the app stores

  • Push notification token — a Firebase Cloud Messaging token for your device, stored in our Firebase (Google Cloud) database so the app can send pick, outcome and BTC-alert notifications. It is deleted automatically when Firebase reports it is no longer valid.
  • Device secret — a random value generated on your phone and kept in the phone's secure storage. Our server stores only a one-way hash of it, used to confirm that settings changes and "Run now" requests come from the owner device. It contains no personal information.
  • App settings — which AI models are enabled, coin list, scan interval, alert thresholds, quiet hours (with your time-zone offset) and the daily spending cap.
  • On-device only — whether you accepted the in-app disclaimer.

The app does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, advertising ID or any other personal data.

Third-party services

  • Binance public market data — our server reads anonymous, keyless public price data. No data about you is sent.
  • DeepInfra — our server sends a market summary (prices and indicators only) to AI models hosted by DeepInfra. No personal data is included.
  • Google Firebase — hosts the database, server functions and push delivery (Firebase privacy).
  • Google Play — the app may ask Google Play whether an update is available.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell or share any data. Trade ideas, their outcomes and model scores are derived from public market data and kept for as long as the app is in use. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device; to have your push token removed from our server immediately, email us.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 18.

Percent & Ratio Helper — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026 · describes Percent & Ratio Helper 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Percent & Ratio Helper collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The numbers you type are used for the calculation on screen and are not saved.

What the app stores

  • Nothing. The app has no saved history and no settings. When you close it, the numbers you typed are gone.
  • The app turns off Android cloud backup; there is no app data to back up in any case.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not request any other permission: no camera, microphone, contacts, location, files or notifications.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not ask for your name, email or any account.

Children

The app is a general-audience maths utility aimed at ages 13 and up. It is not designed for children and collects no data from anyone.

Not advice

The app performs the arithmetic you ask for. It is not financial, tax or legal advice, and it does not decide which percentage applies to your situation.

Changes

If a future version ever collects data (for example, if advertising is added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the Google Play data-safety section will say so.

Shift Rota Calendar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026 · describes Shift Rota Calendar 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Shift Rota Calendar collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Your rota lives only on your phone.

What the app stores, on your phone only

  • Your shift pattern: the shift types you define (a code, a name, a colour, optional start and end times, hours), the repeating cycle, the date it is anchored to, the first day of the week, and any single days you changed by hand.
  • This is kept in the app's private storage on the device. Nothing is uploaded, synced or shared unless you use Backup, which hands the rota as text to an app you choose (email, notes, a cloud drive); from then on that app's privacy policy applies to the copy you shared.
  • The app turns off Android cloud backup, so the system does not copy its data to your Google account. Uninstalling the app deletes the data.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not request any other permission: no calendar, contacts, camera, microphone, location, files or notifications. It does not read or write your phone's calendar.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not ask for your name, email, employer or any account.

Children

The app is a general-audience work-calendar utility aimed at ages 13 and up. It is not designed for children and collects no data from anyone.

Accuracy

The app shows the rota you enter and counts along your cycle from the anchor date using Gregorian calendar dates in your phone's time zone. It does not know your employer's roster, public holidays or pay rules; check shifts and hours that matter against your actual roster.

Changes

If a future version ever collects data (for example, if advertising is added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the Google Play data-safety section will say so.

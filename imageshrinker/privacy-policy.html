Image Shrinker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026 · describes Image Shrinker 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Image Shrinker collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Your photos are resized and compressed on your phone and never leave it.

What the app does with your photos

  • When you tap Pick photos, Android's own photo picker opens. The app receives only the pictures you choose, and only for that one job. It never scans or lists your gallery.
  • The chosen pictures are decoded, resized and re-encoded as new JPEG or WebP copies on the phone. The copies are written to the app's private cache only so they can be handed to the share sheet or saved to a folder you pick. They are deleted when you start again.
  • The app keeps no history, no list of recent files and no copy of any picture. Closing the app discards everything that was not shared or saved.
  • Your original photos are never modified or deleted.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions. Choosing photos and saving files goes through Android's own pickers, which give the app access to only the items you choose. The app does not request internet, storage, camera, microphone, location or contacts access.

Sharing and saving

If you choose Share, Android's share sheet opens and you pick where the file goes; the app we hand it to is governed by that app's own policy. If you choose Save to a folder, the file is written to the folder you select on your phone or a storage provider you have set up there.

Deleting your data

Because nothing leaves your phone and nothing is kept, there is nothing for us to delete. To clear the cache, uninstall the app or use Settings → Apps → Image Shrinker → Storage → Clear cache. Files you shared or saved are yours to delete wherever you put them.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included, and has no ads, links to other apps or ways to contact other people.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above (for example by adding advertising), this page will say so before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

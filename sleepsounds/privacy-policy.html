Sleep Sounds — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Introduction

Sleep Sounds ("we", "our", "the app") is a relaxing ambient sound mixer developed and published by Rizwan Shoaib. Sleep Sounds is designed to help you relax and sleep better using soothing sounds. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and how it's handled. From version 1.2.0 the app is free and supported by a single banner ad served by Google AdMob; the Advertising section below explains exactly what that involves. Versions before 1.2.0 carried no ads.

Data We Access

  • Sound preferences — your selected sounds, volume levels, and timer settings are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted to our servers.
  • Usage and diagnostic data — Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring collect app-interaction events (screens opened, sessions), an app-instance identifier, coarse device information, an approximate location derived from your IP address, performance traces and crash reports with stack traces. This is used to understand how the app is used and to fix faults.
  • Advertising data — described under Advertising.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT collect your personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • We do NOT collect your location from your device's location services
  • We do NOT require account creation or login
  • We do NOT sell your personal data
  • We do NOT record audio — the app only plays sound

Advertising

From version 1.2.0, Sleep Sounds is supported by ads. It displays a single banner ad served by Google AdMob at the bottom of the sound list. There is no full-screen ad, no ad on launch, the ad never plays audio and it never interrupts playback. When the device is offline the sounds still play and no ad is shown.

To request an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app sends the following to Google and its advertising partners:

  • Your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier Android provides for advertising) and app-set ID.
  • Your IP address, from which Google derives an approximate location. Sleep Sounds never asks for location permission.
  • Coarse device and app information — device model, OS version, app version, and interaction with the ad itself.
  • Diagnostics about the ad request, such as whether it loaded.

This data is collected by Google and shared with Google as an advertising partner. It is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google's handling of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the AdMob partner policies.

Consent in Europe. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a Google-certified consent form the first time it runs and records your choice. If you do not consent to personalised advertising, the app requests non-personalised ads instead. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Ad content is limited to Google's "G" (general audience) rating.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — advertising, as described above. Google acts as an advertising partner for this data.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — usage analytics that help us see which sounds and features are used. It collects an app-instance identifier, app-interaction events, coarse device information and an approximate location derived from your IP address. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reporting. It collects stack traces, the app state at the moment of a crash, device metadata and a Crashlytics installation identifier.
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring — app start-up and responsiveness traces, with coarse device information and an IP-derived country.

Google acts as our service provider for the analytics, crash-reporting and performance data above; that data is not shared with advertisers or any other third party. The advertising data is the only data shared with an advertising partner.

Local Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Your sound preferences and mixer settings
  • Saved mixes and timer configurations
  • App settings

This data never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings.

Permissions

  • Internet — Used to request ads and to upload the analytics, crash and performance data described above. Sounds play without it.
  • Advertising ID — Used by Google AdMob to serve ads, as described above.
  • Foreground service / wake lock — Keeps your mix playing with the screen off and shows the lock-screen controls. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your preferences and saved mixes are stored locally on your device only. The analytics, crash, performance and advertising data described above is held by Google under its own retention rules; we operate no servers of our own.

How to delete your data:

  • Go to your device's Settings → Apps → Sleep Sounds → Storage → Clear Data to erase all locally stored app data.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all associated data from your device.
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Ad content is restricted to the general-audience "G" rating.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by the "Last updated" date at the top. The September 30, 2026 update added the Advertising section when the app became ad-supported.

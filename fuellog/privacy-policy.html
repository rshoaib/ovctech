Fuel Log: Mileage & MPG — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · describes Fuel Log 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Fuel Log collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Your fill-ups live only on your phone.

What the app stores, on your phone only

  • Your vehicles: a name you choose, the units you pick (kilometres or miles, litres or gallons, how economy is shown) and an optional currency label.
  • Your fill-ups: the date, the odometer reading, the amount of fuel, what you paid, whether the tank was filled right up, and an optional note.
  • This is kept in a private database inside the app's storage on the device. Nothing is uploaded, synced or shared unless you use Backup, which hands your vehicles and fill-ups as text to an app you choose (email, notes, a cloud drive); from then on that app's privacy policy applies to the copy you shared.
  • The app turns off Android cloud backup, so the system does not copy its data to your Google account. Uninstalling the app deletes the data.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not request any other permission: no location, no Bluetooth, no camera, microphone, contacts, files or notifications. It does not read your car, track your trips or fetch fuel prices.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not ask for your name, email, number plate or any account.

Children

The app is a general-audience utility for drivers, aimed at adults (18 and up). It is not designed for children and collects no data from anyone.

Accuracy

The app does arithmetic on the readings you enter: fuel economy between full tanks, cost per distance, price per litre or gallon and totals. The figures are only as accurate as the odometer and pump readings you type in, and they are not advice about your vehicle, its maintenance or your spending.

Changes

If a future version ever collects data (for example, if advertising is added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the Google Play data-safety section will say so.

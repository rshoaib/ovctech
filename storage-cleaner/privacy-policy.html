Storage Cleaner — Privacy Policy

Published by Rizwan Shoaib (OVC Tech)

Last updated: September 26, 2026

The short version

Storage Cleaner reads the photos and videos on your phone so it can show you which ones are taking up space. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is collected, and there is no account and no server of ours. The app makes no network requests of any kind — the internet permission is removed from the released app entirely, so it is not capable of sending anything anywhere.

What the app reads, and where it goes

  • Your photos and videos. With your permission, the app lists the photos and videos in your library to work out their size, dimensions and date. That listing is held in memory while the app is open and is discarded when you close it. It is never written to a file, never sent anywhere, and never shared with another app.
  • File contents, for duplicate detection only. To be certain that two files really are the same file, the app reads their contents and compares a checksum. The checksum is computed on your device, used only to group identical files during that scan, and then discarded. The contents themselves are never copied, stored or transmitted.
  • Device storage figures. The total and free space shown on the dashboard come from Android itself. They describe the device, not you.

What the app deletes

Only what you select. Nothing is ever pre-selected for you. Before anything is deleted the app shows you exactly how many items and how much space, and Android then shows its own confirmation dialog on top of that — the app cannot delete a photo without the system asking you first. In a set of identical copies, one copy is always kept and cannot be selected for deletion.

Deletion is permanent and is performed by Android, not by us. Depending on your device the files may pass through your gallery's recently-deleted folder; that behaviour belongs to your gallery app, not to Storage Cleaner.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no server.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no advertising ID.
  • No network requests of any kind.
  • No access to other apps' data, caches or system files.
  • No access to your location. The app does not read GPS data out of your photos.
  • No Android cloud backup of the app's data.

Permissions

  • Photos and videos. Required. Listing them is the entire product, and deleting the ones you choose needs the same access.

The app does not request all-files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE). It can only ever see the photos and videos you grant it, and nothing else on the device.

Children

Storage Cleaner is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. If a future version ever collected anything, that would be described here before the version shipped.

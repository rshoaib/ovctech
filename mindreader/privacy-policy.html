Pocket Mind Reader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026 · describes Pocket Mind Reader 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Pocket Mind Reader collects nothing and stores nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.

What the app stores

Nothing. The answers you tap while a trick runs stay in memory only and are gone when you leave the trick or close the app. The app turns off Android cloud backup.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not listen, watch or guess. It requests no microphone, camera, contacts, location, files or notifications permission, and every trick works only from what you tap on screen.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.

Children

Pocket Mind Reader is a general-audience entertainment app for teenagers and adults and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version changes what the app stores or shares, this page will be updated first and the version it describes will change in the line under the title.

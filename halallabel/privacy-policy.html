Halal Label Check — Privacy Policy

Published by Rizwan Shoaib (OVC Tech)

Last updated: October 4, 2026

The short version

Halal Label Check collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere: not your photos, not the text it reads, not usage statistics, not advertising identifiers. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising.

What the app does with the camera

When you press the shutter, the app takes one photo of the ingredients panel you are pointing at, crops it to the guide box, and reads the text with Google ML Kit text recognition. The recognition model is built into the app and runs entirely on your phone.

The photo and the cropped copy are written briefly to the app's private cache so the recogniser can read them, and are deleted as soon as the text has been read. They are never saved to your gallery and never leave the device.

The text that was read is kept in memory only while you are looking at it. The app keeps no history: when you scan another pack or close the app, the previous text is gone.

Permissions

  • Camera — to photograph the ingredients panel. Used only on the scan screen.

The app requests no other permission. In particular it does not have the Android internet permission, so Google ML Kit, which in other apps may report usage metrics to Google, has no way to do so here.

Links you choose to open

Each ingredient result lists the published sources its ruling comes from. If you tap a source link, it opens in your phone's web browser, which is a separate app with its own privacy policy. Halal Label Check itself makes no connection.

What the results are, and are not

Results list ingredients found in the text the app could read, each marked haram, doubtful or halal with the source of that ruling. They are not a halal certification. Text the camera missed is not checked, and Arabic script cannot be read.

Children

The app is suitable for a general audience and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

There is nothing to delete: the app stores no data about you. Uninstalling it removes it completely.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date, and the app's Play Data safety section will be updated to match before that version ships.

MealMatrix — Privacy Policy

Published by Rizwan Shoaib

Last updated: August 23, 2026

The short version

MealMatrix does not collect anything. Your recipes, your weekly plan, your pantry and your shopping list stay on your phone. There is no account, no server and no analytics — and the version on Google Play is built without the Android internet permission, so the app cannot send anything anywhere even if we wanted it to.

What the app stores, and where it goes

  • Your recipes. Titles, ingredients, quantities, cooking notes, prep time and calories — the twelve that ship with the app and any you write yourself.
  • Your meal plan. Which recipe you assigned to which day and meal, and how many servings you intend to cook.
  • Your pantry and grocery list. The staples you have marked as in stock, the generated shopping list, and anything you typed onto it yourself.

All of it is written to a database inside MealMatrix's own private storage area on the device. Uninstalling MealMatrix deletes every bit of it, and there is no copy anywhere else to recover from.

Sharing is something you do, not something the app does

  • Share a grocery list. Tapping the share icon hands a plain-text copy of your list to Android's share sheet, and you choose where it goes. MealMatrix does not pick a destination and does not keep a copy of what you sent.
  • Export a backup. Export writes a readable .json file of your data and hands it to the same share sheet. Where that file ends up is entirely your choice. Import reads a file you select and replaces your current data with it.
  • Contact us. The Contact screen composes a message and opens it in WhatsApp or your email app, with the app version and device model attached so a bug report is useful. Nothing is sent until you press send in that app.

Backups

Android can normally copy an app's data to a user's Google Drive automatically. MealMatrix turns this off (allowBackup=false), so your recipes and plan are not included in a device backup. The Export button above is the deliberate alternative: a copy you make on purpose, and control.

Permissions, and why each one exists

MealMatrix requests no runtime permissions at all. The release build ships with the internet permission stripped out of its manifest. It does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, notifications or your photo library.

Deleting your data

Because nothing leaves your device, there is no server-side account to close and nothing for us to delete on your behalf. You can erase your data at any time, yourself:

  • Inside the app — Settings → Reset to factory presets wipes every recipe you wrote, your plan, your pantry and your grocery list.
  • From Android — Settings → Apps → MealMatrix → Storage → Clear data, or simply uninstall the app.

Either action is immediate and permanent. Export a backup first if you might want any of it back.

Children

MealMatrix is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Third parties

There are none. No advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, no in-app purchases, no third-party accounts, no software development kits that phone home.

Changes

If this policy ever changes — for instance if a future version needed network access — the updated policy will be posted here with a new date, and the app's Play Data safety section will be updated to match before that version ships.

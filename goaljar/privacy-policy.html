Goal Jar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026 · describes Goal Jar 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Goal Jar collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no bank connection, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The goals and amounts you enter stay on your phone, in storage only this app can read.

What the app stores, on your phone only

  • Your goals: the name you give each one, the target amount, a currency code used as a label, and an optional target date.
  • Your entries: each amount you put in or take out, the day, and an optional note you type.

All of it lives in the app's private storage on your device. Deleting an entry or a goal removes it immediately; uninstalling the app removes everything. The app turns off Android cloud backup, so your data is not copied to a Google account.

Sharing is always your choice

  • Share backup writes all your goals and entries as one text file and opens your phone's share sheet. You pick the app to send it with, and you can cancel there. Goal Jar itself sends nothing.
  • Restore reads only the backup text you paste into the app.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not request any other permission: no contacts, camera, microphone, location, files or notifications.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not hold or move money and does not connect to any bank. It records the amounts you type and is not financial advice.

Children

Goal Jar is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version changes what the app stores or shares, this page will be updated first and the version it describes will change in the line under the title.

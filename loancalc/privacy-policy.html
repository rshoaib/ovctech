Loan & Mortgage Calc — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026 · describes Loan & Mortgage Calc 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Loan & Mortgage Calc collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The figures you type (amounts, rates, terms, tax and insurance) are used for the calculation on screen and are not saved.

What the app stores

  • Nothing. The app has no saved history, no profile and no settings. When you close it, the numbers you typed are gone.
  • The app turns off Android cloud backup; there is no app data to back up in any case.

The PDF you can share

On the loan and mortgage screens you can share the amortization schedule as a PDF. The file is generated on your phone, in the app's private cache, and handed to the Android share sheet. It goes only to the app or person you choose there; Loan & Mortgage Calc itself never uploads it and does not keep a copy beyond the cache, which Android clears.

Financial data

Loan amounts, home prices, rates and savings figures are financial information. The app processes them only in memory on your phone, for the result shown on screen (and, if you ask for it, the PDF above), and never stores, transmits or shares them. It does not connect to any bank, lender, credit bureau or rate service, and it does not offer, arrange or facilitate loans.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not request any other permission: no camera, microphone, contacts, location, files, body sensors or notifications.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not ask for your name, email or any account.

Children

The app is a financial calculator aimed at ages 18 and up. It is not designed for children and collects no data from anyone.

Not financial advice

The app applies the standard annuity and compound-interest formulas and shows its working. The results are arithmetic on the numbers you type, not a quote, an offer, a recommendation or financial advice. Lender fees, credit terms, taxes and rate changes are not included; a real offer comes from a lender, and decisions about borrowing or saving are yours to take with proper advice.

Changes

If a future version ever collects data (for example, if advertising is added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the Google Play data-safety section will say so.

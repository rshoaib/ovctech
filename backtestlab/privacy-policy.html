🔬 Backtest Lab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Introduction

Backtest Lab ("we", "our", "the app") is a free crypto strategy backtester developed and published by OVC Tech. The app replays a trading rule you configure over public cryptocurrency price history and reports what that rule would have done. Everything runs on your device. It has no accounts, no ads, and collects no personal information whatsoever. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do.

Data We Access

  • Public market data — the app connects to Binance's public, keyless market-data endpoints to read the list of tradable pairs and historical candlestick (OHLC) data for the coin you choose. These are anonymous, read-only public price feeds. No API key, no account, and no personal or device identifier is sent. The app cannot place orders, cannot access any exchange account, and cannot move funds.
  • Settings and results you create — the strategy you configure and the results of the backtests you run are stored locally on your device only.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ We do NOT collect your personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • ❌ We do NOT have user accounts, logins, or profiles
  • ❌ We do NOT upload any of your data to any server — we operate no server
  • ❌ We do NOT track your location
  • ❌ We do NOT sell or share your data with anyone
  • ❌ We do NOT serve ads of any kind
  • ❌ We do NOT use an advertising ID
  • ❌ We do NOT use third-party analytics, crash reporting, or trackers
  • ❌ We do NOT ask for, store, or transmit exchange API keys or wallet credentials
  • ❌ We do NOT offer trading, brokerage, custody, lending, or any financial service

How Backtests Are Produced

Backtests are calculated inside the app on your device from the public price history described above. There is no signal provider, no analyst, and no remote service involved. The app applies published, standard technical-analysis calculations — moving averages, the Relative Strength Index, and Average True Range — to simulate a rule bar by bar, including trading fees and slippage.

Because everything is computed locally, nothing about which coins you test, which strategies you configure, or how you use the app is ever transmitted anywhere.

Local Storage

The app stores the following on your device, using your device's private on-device storage:

  • The strategy configuration you last set up, so the app reopens where you left it
  • The results of your most recent backtest runs, shown in the Runs tab

This data never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

  • Internet — Required. Used solely to read Binance's public market-data endpoints so the price history is current. It is not used for advertising, analytics, or transmitting anything about you.

The app does not request or use an advertising ID.

Data Retention & Deletion

All app data is stored locally on your device only. We operate no servers, so there is no server-side data to retain or delete.

How long we retain data: local data stays on your device for as long as the app is installed. There is no expiration.

How to delete your data:

  • Go to your device's Settings → Apps → Backtest Lab → Storage → Clear Data to erase all locally stored app data, or
  • Uninstall the app, which permanently deletes all associated data from your device.

Not Financial Advice

Backtest Lab is an educational tool only. A backtest measures one ruleset against one slice of the past; it is not financial, investment, or trading advice, and it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything. OVC Tech is not a licensed financial adviser or broker. Cryptocurrency trading carries substantial risk, including the total loss of your funds. Past results do not predict future returns, and a strategy that performed well in a backtest may lose money in live markets — tuning parameters until a backtest looks good fits the settings to history rather than finding an edge. Any decision you make is your own, and you should consider seeking advice from a qualified professional.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app is intended for a general adult audience.

Tap & Blend — Privacy Policy

Published by OVC Tech · Last updated: 18 September 2026

Tap & Blend collects nothing, stores nothing about your child, and cannot send anything anywhere. It is a phonics toy for children aged about three to six, and it was built so that this page could be this short.

It has no way to reach the internet

This is not a promise about how we behave — it is a property of the app. The release build ships without Android's INTERNET permission. An Android app with no internet permission cannot open a network connection at all. There is no server to talk to, and no way to talk to one.

The only permissions the app carries are the ability to vibrate the phone for gentle feedback, and a standard diagnostic permission Android grants every app.

What we do not collect

  • No name, age, email address, photograph or voice recording
  • No account, sign-in or profile
  • No advertising identifier — there are no ads in this app
  • No analytics, usage statistics, crash reports or telemetry of any kind
  • No location, contacts, photos or files
  • Nothing at all is transmitted off the device, because nothing can be

Children

Tap & Blend is intended for young children and is designed to be handed to one. Because the app collects no data whatsoever and has no network access, it cannot collect personal information from a child, or from anyone else.

There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no links out to other apps or websites, and no way for a child to contact anyone through the app. The one screen intended for a grown-up sits behind a parent gate.

What stays on the device

The app remembers only which word you were last looking at, in its own private storage on the phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it. It never leaves the device.

Changes

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this page, this page changes first, and the date at the top changes with it.

Hifz Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026 · describes Hifz Tracker 1.0.0

The short version

Hifz Tracker collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Everything you record stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

Hifz Tracker keeps one small file in its private storage on your device. It contains:

  • which pages you have marked as memorised, and the day you logged each sabaq;
  • the pages you flagged as weak and when each is next due;
  • your manzil position, cycle length and other settings;
  • a history of the days you logged sabaq, sabqi, manzil and weak-page reviews.

The app contains no Quran text and no audio, and never asks for your name, email or any other personal detail. Android's automatic cloud backup is switched off for this app.

Backup files

If you choose Save backup file, the app writes that same data as a JSON file into a folder you pick. Where that file goes afterwards (your phone, Google Drive, a message to yourself) is your choice; we never receive it. Import backup reads a file you pick and replaces the data in the app after asking you to confirm.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): for the daily reminder you can switch on in Settings. It is scheduled and shown entirely on your phone.
  • Run at startup: so the reminder is set again after your phone restarts.

The app does not request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions.

Deleting your data

Because nothing leaves your phone, there is nothing for us to delete. To remove everything, uninstall the app or use Settings → Apps → Hifz Tracker → Storage → Clear data. Backup files you saved are yours to delete wherever you put them.

Children

Many hifz students are children. The app collects no data from anyone, children included, and has no ads, links to other apps or ways to contact other people.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above, this page will say so before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

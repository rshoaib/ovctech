Prayer times look wrong

Open Settings and pick the calculation method your local mosque uses (for example Umm Al-Qura in Saudi Arabia, Qatar in Qatar, ISNA in North America, Karachi in Pakistan, Diyanet in Turkey), and set Asr to Standard or Hanafi. Times are calculated from your location, so make sure location access is allowed for Azan Time.

I don't hear the adhan (iPhone)

  • Check that notifications are allowed: iPhone Settings → Notifications → Azan Time → Allow Notifications, with Sounds on.
  • Focus modes and Silent mode can mute alerts. Allow Azan Time in your Focus settings if you want alerts during Focus.
  • iOS limits a notification sound to 30 seconds, so the alert plays the opening of the adhan. With the app open, the full adhan plays.
  • Open the app at least once every week or so: iOS keeps a limited number of scheduled alerts, and opening the app schedules the next days.

I don't hear the adhan (Android)

Open Settings → Notification health in the app. It shows what is blocking alerts (notifications, exact alarms, battery optimisation) with a one-tap fix for each.

The Qibla compass is off

Hold the phone flat, away from metal objects, magnets and laptops, and move it in a figure-of-eight a few times to calibrate the compass. The arrow turns green when you face the Qibla.

Your data

There is no account. Your prayer log, tasbih count and settings stay on your phone; deleting the app deletes them. Read the privacy policy for the details, including what the app sends to OpenStreetMap, the radio stations and Google Firebase.

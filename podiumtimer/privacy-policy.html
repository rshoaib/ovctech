Podium Timer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026 · describes Podium Timer 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Podium Timer collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The speaker names and timings you enter stay on your phone, in storage only this app can read.

What the app stores, on your phone only

  • Your timing log: the speaker name you typed (optional), which timing set was used, its green, amber and red times, the grace setting, the time taken and when it was saved.
  • Your settings: whether the phone vibrates at each card, whether the time is shown while timing, the grace window, and your custom green, amber and red times.

All of it lives in the app's private storage on your device. Removing a row or clearing the log deletes it immediately; uninstalling the app removes everything. The app turns off Android cloud backup, so your data is not copied to a Google account.

Sharing is always your choice

  • Share the timer's report writes a plain-text list of the timed speeches and opens your phone's share sheet. You pick the app to send it with, and you can cancel there. Podium Timer itself sends nothing.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • Its only permission is VIBRATE, used for the buzz when the card changes. You can turn the buzz off in Settings. It requests no contacts, camera, microphone, location, files or notifications.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.

Children

Podium Timer is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version changes what the app stores or shares, this page will be updated first and the version it describes will change in the line under the title.

Zakat Calc — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026 · describes Zakat Calc 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Zakat Calc collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The amounts you type are stored on your phone and never leave it.

What the app stores

  • The calculator form as you last left it: your currency label, the gold and silver prices you typed, the nisab you chose, and the amounts for cash, gold, silver, investments, business stock, money owed to you and debts.
  • Each result you choose to save: the date, the currency label, your net zakatable wealth, the nisab, the zakat figure and which nisab was used.
  • Everything is kept in the app's private storage on this phone. No other app can read it, and the app turns off Android cloud backup, so it is not copied to any cloud.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions. It does not request internet, storage, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar or location access. It does not fetch gold or silver prices; you type them.

Deleting your data

Use Delete everything on the About screen, or delete single saved results on the History screen. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Apps → Zakat Calc → Storage → Clear storage, removes everything the app ever stored. Because nothing leaves your phone, there is nothing for us to delete.

Children

The app is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, children included, and has no ads, links to other apps or ways to contact other people.

Not a religious ruling

Zakat Calc gives an estimate using the common 2.5% method. It does not give religious rulings; for your own situation, ask a scholar you trust.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above (for example by adding advertising), this page will say so before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

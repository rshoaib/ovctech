MyCash — Privacy Policy

Simple, local-only cash book

Last updated: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the MyCash mobile application (the “App”, package name com.ovctech.mycash), developed by Rizwan Shoaib (“we”, “us”, “our”), handles your information. In short: your cash book stays on your phone, and the app is free because it shows a banner ad supplied by Google AdMob. Serving that ad means some technical information about your device is collected by Google — never your entries.

1. Advertising

MyCash is free and supported by ads. It displays a single banner ad served by Google AdMob. There is no full-screen ad and no ad on launch.

To request an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the App sends the following to Google and its advertising partners:

  • Your device’s advertising ID (a resettable identifier Android provides for advertising) and app-set ID.
  • Coarse device and app information — device model, operating system version, language, screen size, and the fact that the request came from MyCash.
  • Your IP address, from which Google can estimate an approximate (city-level) location.
  • Interactions with the ad and with the screen showing it, plus basic diagnostic and performance data, used to count impressions, detect invalid traffic and fix faults.

This data is collected by Google and shared with Google as an advertising partner, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. See Google Privacy & Terms and the AdMob partner policies.

Consent in Europe. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the App shows a Google-certified consent form on first run and records your choice. If you do not consent to personalised advertising, the App requests non-personalised ads instead. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

2. What We Do Not Do

Beyond the advertising described above, the App has:

  • No account creation, login, or sign-up.
  • No backend servers or cloud storage operated by us.
  • No analytics SDK of our own and no other third-party trackers.
  • No access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, or photos.

Earlier versions of MyCash carried no ads at all. This policy was updated when that changed.

3. Your Cash Book Stays on Your Device

Any data you enter — such as cash in/out entries, amounts, descriptions, dates, and currency preferences — is stored only on your device using the device’s local storage (AsyncStorage). This information never leaves your phone and is never sent to us or any third party.

Because this data is stored locally, it remains under your control. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, this information is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered by us.

4. Sharing to WhatsApp and Other Apps

MyCash includes an optional feature to share an individual entry as a text message through WhatsApp or your device’s standard share sheet. This action is performed entirely by you and only when you tap “Share”. The content is handed off to the app you choose; we do not see, intercept, or store anything you share. The privacy practices of WhatsApp or any other app you select are governed by their own policies.

5. Permissions

MyCash does not request access to sensitive device permissions such as precise location, contacts, camera, or microphone. It declares the INTERNET permission and the advertising-ID permission, both solely so the banner ad described above can be requested. Your entries never travel over that connection.

6. Children’s Privacy

MyCash is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad content is restricted to a general-audience rating.

7. Data Security

Your cash-book data is stored only on your own device and is never transmitted, so its security depends on your device’s own protections (e.g. screen lock and OS-level security). We recommend keeping your device secured. The advertising data described in section 1 is sent to Google over an encrypted (TLS) connection.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

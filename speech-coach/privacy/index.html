Speech Coach — Privacy Policy

Published by Rizwan Shoaib (OVC Tech)

Last updated: September 17, 2026

The short version

Speech Coach does not collect anything. Your scripts, your recorded takes, their transcripts and your pace figures stay on your phone. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. The one place a recording goes outside the app is your own phone's gallery, and only because you recorded it there to watch it back.

What the app records, and where it goes

  • Scripts. The text you write or paste is stored in the app's own private database on the device. It is never uploaded.
  • Video and audio. When you press record, the app films you with the front camera and microphone. The file is saved to Speech Coach's private storage area, and a copy is added to your phone's gallery so you can watch or share it with any app you choose. The app only ever adds to the gallery; it does not read, list or modify anything already in it.
  • Speech transcript. While you record, your phone's built-in offline speech recognition converts what you say to text so the teleprompter can follow your voice and the app can work out your words per minute. This runs entirely on the device. Speech Coach explicitly requests on-device recognition and never falls back to a network recogniser — if no offline language pack is installed, the feature is switched off rather than sent elsewhere.
  • Your takes. Date, length, word count, pace and the transcript for each take are saved in the app's local database alongside the script they belong to.

All of this lives only on your phone. Uninstalling Speech Coach deletes every script, take and number held by the app. Copies you exported to the gallery are yours and remain there.

Backups

Android can normally copy an app's data to a user's Google Drive automatically. Speech Coach turns this off (allowBackup=false), so your scripts, takes and transcripts are not included in a device backup.

Permissions, and why each one exists

  • Camera — to film your take. Used only while the studio screen is open.
  • Microphone — to record your voice and, via your phone's offline speech recognition, to follow your script and measure your pace.
  • Photos and videos — write-only access, to add finished takes to your gallery. This needs no runtime prompt on Android 13 and later. On Android 12 and earlier the app does not hold the legacy storage permission, so the gallery export is skipped there and the take stays inside the app.
  • Keep awake — so the screen does not lock in the middle of a take.
  • Internet — declared by the app framework, but Speech Coach has no server, no analytics and no ads, and makes no network requests of its own.

The app does not request location, contacts, or read access to your photo library.

Contact & feedback

The in-app contact screen hands a prefilled message to WhatsApp or your email app, which you then send yourself. The message carries the app version, your device model and Android version, and the recognition language you chose — nothing from your scripts, takes or transcripts.

Children

Speech Coach is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Third parties

There are none. No advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party accounts. Speech recognition is performed by the software already built into your phone, not by a service Speech Coach sends your audio to.

Changes

If this policy ever changes — for instance if a future version needed network access — the updated policy will be posted here with a new date, and the app's Play Data safety section will be updated to match before that version ships.

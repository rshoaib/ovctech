Arithmetic Reflex — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026 · describes Arithmetic Reflex 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Arithmetic Reflex collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.

What is stored, and where

  • Your best score.
  • Your daily streak and how many rounds you finished today.
  • This is kept only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never sent anywhere, and no other app can read it.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions: no internet, storage, camera, microphone, location or contacts access.

Updates

When a newer version is available, the app may ask the Google Play app on your phone to show its standard update prompt. That check is handled by Google Play under Google's own policies; the app sends nothing of its own.

Deleting your data

To erase your best score and streak, use Settings → Apps → Arithmetic Reflex → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. There is nothing on any server for us to delete.

Children

Arithmetic Reflex is a simple maths game that people of any age, including children, may enjoy. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included: no data leaves the phone. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no links out of the app and no way to contact other people.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above (for example by adding advertising), this page will say so before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

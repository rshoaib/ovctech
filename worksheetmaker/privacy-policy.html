Maths Worksheet Maker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026 · describes Maths Worksheet Maker 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Maths Worksheet Maker collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The worksheets it generates leave the phone only when you share or copy them yourself.

What the app stores

  • Your last worksheet settings (the topics, level, number of questions and the optional title you typed), kept in the app's private storage on your phone so the next worksheet starts from them.
  • When you export a worksheet or answer-key PDF, the file is written to the app's cache folder and handed to the share sheet; the app you choose (a printer, email, a drive) receives the file. Copying puts the questions on the clipboard.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. The app turns off Android cloud backup.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. The release build declares no INTERNET permission, so the operating system will not let it make a network request.
  • It does not request any runtime permission: no storage, contacts, location, camera or microphone. Sharing and clipboard access go through the Android share sheet and clipboard APIs, which need no permission.
  • It does not show ads, use analytics or crash reporting, or include any third-party SDK that collects data.
  • It does not ask for your name, email, pupils' names or any account. The name, date and score lines on a printed worksheet are blank for whoever fills it in on paper.

Children

The app is a tool for teachers, tutors and parents (18 and over) who prepare practice sheets. It is not designed to be used by children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version ever collects data (for example, if advertising is added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the Google Play data-safety section will say so.

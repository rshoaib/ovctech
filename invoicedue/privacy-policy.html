Invoice Due — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026 · describes Invoice Due 1.0.0, the version on Google Play

The short version

Invoice Due collects nothing. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The invoices you enter are stored on your phone and never leave it unless you export them yourself.

What the app stores

  • Each invoice you add: the client name, an optional invoice number, the amount and currency, the invoice date, the payment terms, an optional note, and the day you marked it paid. Nothing else.
  • Two preferences: the currency and the payment terms you used last, so the next invoice starts with them filled in.
  • Everything is kept in the app's private storage on this phone. No other app can read it, and it is not backed up to any cloud by the app.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions. It does not request internet, storage, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar or location access.

Export

If you choose Export as CSV, the app hands your invoice list as plain text to Android's share sheet and you pick where it goes. The app you send it to is governed by that app's own policy. Nothing is exported unless you tap that button.

Deleting your data

Delete any invoice from its own screen. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Apps → Invoice Due → Storage → Clear storage, removes everything the app ever stored. Because nothing leaves your phone, there is nothing for us to delete.

Not advice

The app does date arithmetic on the payment terms you choose. It does not give legal, tax or accounting advice; your invoice or contract decides the terms.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included, and has no ads, links to other apps or ways to contact other people.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above (for example by adding advertising), this page will say so before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
